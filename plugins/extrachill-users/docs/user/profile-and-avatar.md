# Your profile and avatar

Your profile is a place to introduce yourself and choose how you appear around Extra Chill.

## Open your profile

1. Sign in.
2. Open the account menu by selecting your avatar.
3. Choose **View Profile** to see your public profile, or **Edit Profile** to make changes.

The menu also includes **Settings** for account choices such as your Local Scene and notification preferences.

## Update your profile

On **Edit Profile**, update the profile details offered on the page and save your changes. Your display name and username help other people recognize you. Your profile is available across Extra Chill sites through the same account.

You can change your Local Scene and its visibility in account settings. A public Local Scene can be seen by other people; choose **Private** if you do not want it shown publicly.

## Change your avatar

Choose **Edit Profile**, add or change your profile picture, and select **Save Profile Changes**. Your picture shows in your account menu, beside your community posts, and on your public profile across Extra Chill sites. If you never add one, a default image is shown instead.

You must be signed in to edit your profile. If changes do not appear, refresh the profile and check that you saved the edit.
