# Two-Step Sign-In

Two-step sign-in protects your account with a short code on top of your password. Even if someone learns your password, they can't sign in without the code. It works the same on the Extra Chill website and in the Extra Chill app, and one setting covers every Extra Chill site.

Two-step sign-in is optional. If you don't see it in your settings yet, it isn't available for your account right now.

## Turn On Two-Step Sign-In

1. Sign in and open your [account settings](https://community.extrachill.com/settings#tab-security). You can also get there from **Settings** in your account menu.
2. Open the **Security** tab and find **Two-step sign-in**.
3. Choose how you want to get your code: an authenticator app or email. You can turn on both.

The top of the section tells you whether two-step sign-in is on and which way of getting a code is used first.

Sometimes Extra Chill asks you to **Confirm it's you** before a change is saved. Enter a code from a method you already use, or choose **Email me a code**, then select **Confirm**. Your change saves right after.

## Use an Authenticator App

An authenticator app on your phone makes a new 6-digit code every 30 seconds. Use an authenticator app that can create sign-in codes.

1. In the **Two-step sign-in** section, select **Set up authenticator app**.
2. Open your authenticator app and add an account. Scan the square code on the screen with your phone. If you are setting up on the same phone, select **Open in authenticator app**, or select **Copy setup key** and paste the key into the app.
3. Type the 6-digit code your app shows into the box, then select **Turn on**.

If setup takes too long, the key expires. Start again to get a new one.

## Use Email Codes

Email codes send a code to your account email address each time you sign in.

1. In the **Two-step sign-in** section, select **Turn on email codes**.
2. When you sign in, check your inbox for the code. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or select **Resend code**.

Make sure the email address on your account is one you can open. You can change it from the **Security** tab. See [Account settings](https://docs.extrachill.com/accounts/account-settings).

If you use both an authenticator app and email codes, select **Use this first** next to the one you want Extra Chill to ask for first. You can always switch to the other one while signing in.

## Save Your Backup Codes

Backup codes let you sign in if you lose your phone or can't get an email. Each code works once.

1. In the **Backup codes** part of the section, select **Get backup codes**.
2. Select **Copy codes** or **Download codes**, and keep them somewhere safe, like a password manager or a printed page.
3. Select **I saved them**.

Your codes are only shown once. The section shows how many unused codes you have left. When you run low, select **Get new backup codes**. Your old codes stop working as soon as you get new ones, so Extra Chill asks you to confirm first.

## Sign In With a Code

1. Sign in with your email or username and password as usual, on the website or in the app.
2. Enter the code from your authenticator app, the code we emailed you, or one of your backup codes.
3. Select **Verify**.

To get a code a different way, choose another option below the code box, like using a backup code instead. If you enter too many wrong codes, wait a moment before you try again.

## Turn It Off

1. Open the **Security** tab in your [account settings](https://community.extrachill.com/settings#tab-security).
2. Select **Turn off** next to the method you want to stop using.
3. Confirm your choice.

If it's your last way to get a code, your backup codes stop working too, and you sign in with just your password.

## If You Lose Your Phone

1. Sign in with one of your backup codes, or choose email codes if you turned them on.
2. Open the **Security** tab, turn off the authenticator app, and set it up again on your new phone.
3. Get new backup codes if you used one.

If you can't sign in at all, [contact the Extra Chill team](https://extrachill.com/contact-us/). For your safety, the team checks that the account is yours before helping.
