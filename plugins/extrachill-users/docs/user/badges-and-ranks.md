# Badges and ranks

Badges and ranks help other people recognize your role and activity in the Extra Chill community.

## Profile badges

Your profile may show badges for roles such as **Artist**, **Music Industry Professional**, or **Extra Chill Team Member**. These identify account roles; they are not choices you add yourself from the profile editor. If a badge does not match your role, check the choices you made during account setup or contact Extra Chill support.

A gold heart is the **Extra Chill Supporter** badge. It appears for everyone who has a [lifetime membership](lifetime-membership.md), as a thank-you for helping keep Extra Chill independent.

## Community rank

Your rank reflects points earned through activity across Extra Chill. As your point total grows, your rank can move up to a new tier. You do not need to apply for each tier.

Ranks and badges are different: a badge identifies a role, while a rank reflects accumulated community activity. The rank name and progress shown on your account are the clearest guide to your current standing.
