# Notifications and email digests

Choose how you hear about activity on your account. You can read notifications on the site and, when enabled, get a summary of unread notifications by email.

## Open notification settings

1. Sign in.
2. Open your account menu by selecting your avatar.
3. Choose **Settings** and open the **Notifications** tab.
4. Change your choices and choose **Save Preferences**.

Your settings apply to your own account. You must be signed in to change them.

## Email digests

The unread-notification email digest brings unread account notifications together in an email. Turn off notification emails in your settings if you do not want these summaries. A one-click unsubscribe link in an email is another way to stop the digest. You can turn email notifications back on in your settings.

The digest is a summary, not an instant alert. New activity may appear first in the site's **Notifications** area. A notification is included only once; if it was already included in an earlier digest, it will not trigger another digest by itself.

## Weekly Local Scene digest

Get one email a week with upcoming shows in your [Local Scene](https://docs.extrachill.com/accounts/local-scene). It covers shows in the next seven days, starting with the shows you marked as going under **You're Going**, then **More This Week**. You also get an in-app notice that says **Your Local Scene weekly picks are ready**. If there are no upcoming shows in your city that week, nothing is sent.

1. Set a Local Scene first. In **Settings**, open **Account Details** and pick your city. Until you do, the **Notifications** tab asks you to choose one.
2. Open the **Notifications** tab.
3. Check the weekly Local Scene digest option for your city.
4. Choose **Save Preferences**.

The email part also needs **Email notifications** turned on. You can also sign up from an event page that offers **Get this week's shows** in that city **by email**. If you have no Local Scene yet, signing up there also makes that city your Local Scene.

The digest always covers the city you signed up for, even if your Local Scene is a different city. Changing your Local Scene later doesn't stop or move a digest you already have. To switch cities, unsubscribe from the old city and turn the digest on for your new one.

To stop the digest, uncheck it in the **Notifications** tab, choose **Unsubscribe** next to it in the **Subscriptions** tab, or use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of the email. Unsubscribing stops the weekly email and its in-app notice for that city only. Your saved Local Scene and your other choices stay the same. Unsubscribe links in these emails stop working after 30 days, so use **Settings** for older emails.

## Your subscriptions

The **Subscriptions** tab in **Settings** lists the updates you asked Extra Chill to send you, such as your weekly Local Scene digest for each city you signed up for. Only you can see this list. Choose **Unsubscribe** next to anything you no longer want.

## On-site and device notifications

Select the notification bell or **Notifications** area to see account activity on the site. Some accounts may also have a choice for push notifications on a registered device, and a choice about automatically receiving replies on conversations you take part in. These options only help if the relevant device or conversation is available to your account.

If you expected an email but did not get one, check your spam folder, confirm you are signed in to the right account, and make sure email notifications are enabled. If you opted out from an email link, turn them back on in **Settings**.
