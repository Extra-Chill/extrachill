# Notifications and email digests

Choose how you hear about activity on your account. You can read notifications on the site and, when enabled, get a summary of unread notifications by email.

## Open notification settings

1. Sign in.
2. Open your account menu by selecting your avatar.
3. Choose **Settings** and open the notification preferences.
4. Change your choices and save them.

Your settings apply to your own account. You must be signed in to change them.

## Email digests

The unread-notification email digest brings unread account notifications together in an email. Turn off notification emails in your settings if you do not want these summaries. A one-click unsubscribe link in an email is another way to stop the digest. You can turn email notifications back on in your settings.

The digest is a summary, not an instant alert. New activity may appear first in the site's **Notifications** area.

## On-site and device notifications

Select the notification bell or **Notifications** area to see account activity on the site. Some accounts may also have a choice for push notifications on a registered device, and a choice about automatically receiving replies on conversations you take part in. These options only help if the relevant device or conversation is available to your account.

If you expected an email but did not get one, check your spam folder, confirm you are signed in to the right account, and make sure email notifications are enabled. If you opted out from an email link, turn them back on in **Settings**.
