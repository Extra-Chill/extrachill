# Set up your account

A few choices help make your Extra Chill profile yours and make it easier to find music and people near you.

## Complete first-time setup

After you create an account, the welcome page asks you to set up your profile.

1. Choose a username. Use 3–60 characters made from letters, numbers, hyphens, or underscores.
2. Add a **Local Scene** if you want. Search for a city or region. This helps you find live music nearby, and you can change it later.
3. Choose whether your Local Scene is **Public** or **Private**.
4. Answer the optional prompts about what your Link Page is for, if they appear, and whether you are a musician or work in the music industry.
5. Select **Complete Setup**.

You can skip the Local Scene. If you do not add one during setup, you can add it later in **Settings**. If you do not see search suggestions, you can return to this choice later.

## What happens next

Your account setup is complete and you can continue to the page you were trying to visit, or to the community site. You can update your account and profile choices later. You need to be signed in to complete setup or change your personal settings.
