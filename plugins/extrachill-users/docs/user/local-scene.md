# Your Local Scene

Your Local Scene is the city or region you call home for live music. Setting one helps you meet people nearby, find shows in your area, and get a weekly email of what is happening around you.

## What your Local Scene does

- **Meet people nearby.** Every Local Scene has its own **People in** page on the community site, such as [People in Charleston, South Carolina](https://community.extrachill.com/local-scene/charleston). It lists members who share that Local Scene publicly, with their picture and title. Each page also links to live music in that city on the events calendar.
- **Show where you are from.** When your Local Scene is public, your profile shows **Local Scene:** with your city. Your city also appears beside your forum posts. Both link to that city's **People in** page.
- **Find shows faster.** The [events calendar](https://events.extrachill.com) can focus on your city and show **Showing events for** your Local Scene. Choose **Explore all locations** to look elsewhere, then **Use my Local Scene** to come back. This works on any device where you are signed in.
- **Get weekly picks.** You can turn on a weekly email of upcoming shows in your Local Scene. The email stays with the city you signed up for, so changing your Local Scene later doesn't stop or move it. See [Notifications and email digests](https://docs.extrachill.com/accounts/notifications-and-digests).

## Public or private

You choose who can see your Local Scene.

- **Public:** your city appears on your profile and beside your forum posts, and you are listed on your city's **People in** page.
- **Private:** your city still shapes your events experience and weekly picks, but it is hidden from your profile and forum posts. You are not listed on the **People in** page.

New Local Scenes are public unless you turn that choice off.

## Set or change your Local Scene

1. Sign in.
2. Open your account menu by selecting your avatar, then choose **Settings**.
3. Stay on the **Account Details** tab.
4. In **Local Scene**, search for your city or region and pick it from the list. To remove your Local Scene, clear the field.
5. Check or uncheck **Show my Local Scene publicly**.
6. Choose **Save Account Details**.

You can also set your Local Scene during account setup. If you skip it, the community site may show a **Find your Local Scene** reminder. You can pick your city right there, or choose **Dismiss** to hide the reminder.

## If something looks wrong

If your city does not show up in the search, try a nearby larger city or the region name. If you see "Local Scene search is temporarily unavailable," try again a little later. If you do not appear on your city's **People in** page, make sure **Show my Local Scene publicly** is checked and saved.
