# Create an account or sign in

An Extra Chill account gives you one sign-in for every Extra Chill site. You do not need a separate account for each site.

## Create your account

1. Choose **Register** on an Extra Chill site.
2. Follow the sign-up steps shown on the page.
3. After creating your account, complete the profile setup when prompted.

Use an email address you can access. You may also see an option to **Sign in with Google**. Choose that option and use the Google account connected to your Extra Chill account.

## Sign in

Choose **Log In** or **Sign in** and enter the account details requested. If you are on another Extra Chill site, sign in with the same account you already use.

## If you cannot get in

- **Forgot your password?** Choose the password reset option on the sign-in page and follow the instructions sent to your email. Check your spam folder if the message does not arrive.
- **Sign-in is blocked or keeps failing?** Check that you are using the email address for your account and the right sign-in method. If you created or use the account with Google, try **Sign in with Google**. If the page still blocks you, contact Extra Chill for help.
- **Google sign-in takes you to a different page?** Continue there and return to the site you started from after signing in.

Signing in is all you need to use your account across Extra Chill sites; you do not need to register again on each one.
