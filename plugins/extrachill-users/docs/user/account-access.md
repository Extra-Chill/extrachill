# Create an account or sign in

An Extra Chill account gives you one sign-in for every Extra Chill site. You do not need a separate account for each site.

## Create your account

1. Choose **Register** on an Extra Chill site. You can create an account with an email address and password, or choose **Sign up with Google** if that option appears.
2. Enter an email address you can access and create a password of at least 8 characters. Enter the password again to confirm it.
3. Submit the form. Complete first-time setup by choosing a username and any profile choices offered.

You may also see **Sign up with Google** when registering. Choose it to use your Google account. Google sign-in can connect to an existing account with the same email address. If it does not, choose **Log In** with your password to connect your Google account. When signing in, the Google option is **Continue with Google**; use the Google account connected to your Extra Chill account.

## Sign in

Choose **Log In**, enter your username or email address and password, then submit the form. If you are on another Extra Chill site, sign in with the same account you already use. When Google sign-in is available, choose **Continue with Google** instead.

## If you cannot get in

- **Forgot your password?** Choose the password reset option on the sign-in page and follow the instructions sent to your email. Check your spam folder if the message does not arrive.
- **Sign-in is blocked or keeps failing?** Check that you are using your username or email address and the right sign-in method. If you use Google to access the account, choose **Continue with Google**. If the page says there have been too many failed attempts, wait 15 minutes before trying again. If you still cannot sign in, contact Extra Chill for help.
- **Google sign-in takes you to a different page?** Continue there and return to the site you started from after signing in.

Signing in is all you need to use your account across Extra Chill sites; you do not need to register again on each one.
