# Frequently asked questions

## Do I need a different account for each Extra Chill site?

No. One Extra Chill account works across every Extra Chill site. Sign in with the same account wherever you go.

## I forgot my password. What should I do?

Choose the password reset option on the sign-in page, enter your account email or username, and follow the reset message. Check spam if it does not arrive. If a reset link has expired, request a new one.

## Why can I not sign in?

Make sure the email or username is correct and use the sign-in method linked to your account. Try **Sign in with Google** if that is how you normally access your account. If the page says access is blocked or you still cannot sign in, contact Extra Chill support.

## Where can I update my profile and notification choices?

Select your avatar after signing in. **Edit Profile** opens profile changes, and **Settings** has account choices such as your Local Scene and notification preferences.

## What does lifetime membership include?

It's a one-time way to support Extra Chill. You get the gold-heart Extra Chill Supporter badge and ad-free browsing across every Extra Chill site. Be sure to sign in with the account that has the membership.

## Why did I not receive a digest email?

Check spam, confirm you are checking the email address for your account, and make sure notification emails are enabled in **Settings**. The digest summarizes unread notifications; it is not an instant alert.
