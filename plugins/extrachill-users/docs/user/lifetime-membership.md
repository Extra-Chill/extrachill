# Lifetime membership

Lifetime membership gives you ad-free access across every Extra Chill site, with one membership tied to your Extra Chill account.

You can buy a lifetime membership once in the [Extra Chill Shop](https://shop.extrachill.com). When you buy it, enter the community username that should receive the membership; it fills in your own username if you are signed in.

If your account has lifetime membership, sign in with that account to use the benefit across the sites. If ads still appear, first check that you are signed in to the account that holds the membership. If you need help confirming the membership on your account, contact Extra Chill support.
