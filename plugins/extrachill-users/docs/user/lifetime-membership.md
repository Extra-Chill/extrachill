# Lifetime membership

A lifetime membership is how you show your support for Extra Chill. It's a one-time purchase that helps keep an independent music platform running, with no subscription to manage.

## What you get

- **The Extra Chill Supporter badge.** A gold heart appears next to your name in the community, on your profile, and on the leaderboard, so people can see you back the scene.
- **No ads, anywhere.** Browse every Extra Chill site ad-free while you're signed in.

Both stay with your account for good.

## Get a membership

Buy a lifetime membership once in the [Extra Chill Shop](https://shop.extrachill.com). At checkout, enter the community username that should receive the membership; it fills in your own username if you are signed in. You can also buy one as a gift by entering someone else's username.

## If something looks wrong

If you don't see your badge or ads still appear, first check that you are signed in to the account that holds the membership. If you need help confirming the membership on your account, contact Extra Chill support.
