# Account settings

Settings is where you control your name, sign-in details, privacy, emails, and connected devices. One account works across every Extra Chill site, so changes here apply everywhere.

## Open Settings

1. Sign in.
2. Open your account menu by selecting your avatar.
3. Choose **Settings**.

Settings has five tabs: **Account Details**, **Security**, **Subscriptions**, **Notifications**, and **Artist Platform**. Tabs with choices to save have their own save button.

## Account Details

Use this tab for how you appear and what other people can see.

- **First Name** and **Last Name.** Your real name, if you want to add it.
- **Display Name.** Choose how your name appears around Extra Chill from the list of options.
- **Local Scene.** Search for your city or region. Check **Show my Local Scene publicly** to show it on your profile and forum posts. See [Your Local Scene](https://docs.extrachill.com/accounts/local-scene).
- **Show my concert history publicly.** When this is off, only you and Extra Chill administrators can see your tracked shows and concert stats.
- **Show me in event attendee lists.** When this is off, you still count toward an event's total, but your name and picture stay hidden from the attendee list.

Choose **Save Account Details** when you are done.

## Change your email address

1. Open the **Security** tab.
2. Type your new address in **New Email Address**.
3. Choose **Change Email**.
4. Open the email sent to your new address and choose **Confirm Email Change**.

Until you confirm, Settings shows **Email change pending** with the new address, and your current email keeps working. The new address must be different from your current one and not already used by another account. If the confirmation email does not arrive, check your spam folder, then try again. If you did not ask for the change, ignore the email and nothing changes.

## Change your password

1. Open the **Security** tab.
2. Enter your **Current Password**.
3. Enter your **New Password** twice, in **New Password** and **Confirm New Password**.
4. Choose **Change Password**.

Your new password must be at least 8 characters and cannot include a backslash. If you forgot your current password, sign out and use the password reset option on the sign-in page instead.

## Connected apps and devices

The **Connected Apps** list on the **Security** tab shows apps and devices that can use your account. Each one shows when it was created and last used.

- Choose **Revoke** to cut off an app or device you no longer use or do not recognize.
- The device you are using now is marked **(this device)**. To end that session, sign out instead.

Revoking stops new activity right away, but a session already in progress can keep working for up to 15 minutes.

## Notifications

The **Notifications** tab controls how Extra Chill keeps you in the loop:

- **Email notifications** sends email digests of your notifications.
- **Push notifications** sends replies, mentions and requests to your phone through the Extra Chill app.
- **Auto-subscribe to replies** signs you up for reply notifications on conversations you join.
- **Weekly Local Scene digest** sends weekly show picks for your Local Scene.

Choose **Save Preferences** after you make changes. Learn more in [Notifications and email digests](https://docs.extrachill.com/accounts/notifications-and-digests).

## Subscriptions

The **Subscriptions** tab lists the updates you asked Extra Chill to send you, such as your weekly Local Scene digest. These are private. Choose **Unsubscribe** next to anything you no longer want. If you have none, the tab says so.

## Artist Platform

The **Artist Platform** tab is where musicians and music industry folks ask for access to create artist profiles and Link Pages. Choose **I am a musician** or **I work in the music industry**, then choose **Request Access**. While your request waits for review, the tab shows that it is pending. Once you are approved, the tab links to your artist tools. See [Artist access requirements](https://docs.extrachill.com/artist-platform/artist-access-requirements).

## Getting a copy of your data

There is no download button for your account data yet. To ask for a copy, send a request through the [contact form](https://extrachill.com/contact-us) from the email address on your account. The Extra Chill team will follow up with you.

## Deleting your account

To close your account for good, see [Deleting your account](https://docs.extrachill.com/accounts/deleting-your-account).
