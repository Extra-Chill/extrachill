# Set up your profile

A profile helps other members recognize you and learn about your music or other work. Your public profile can show your picture, bio, links, community activity, and rank.

## Update your profile

1. Sign in and open your profile.
2. Choose **Edit Profile**.
3. Add or change your picture, display name, custom title, and short bio.
4. Add your area and any links you want to share, such as a website or music page. You can add more than one link and change their order.
5. Save your changes, then choose **View Public Profile** to see what other members can see.

Your picture also appears beside your community posts. If you leave the custom title blank, it appears as **Extra Chillian**. You can manage artist profiles you have access to when that option appears on your profile.

## Your activity calendar

Your profile shows a **Contribution Activity** calendar below your **About** section. It is a grid of small squares, one for each day, so anyone can see how active you have been at a glance. Darker squares mean more activity that day. Point at a square to see how many contributions you made on that date.

These count as contributions:

- Starting a forum topic or posting a reply.
- Publishing an article on the main Extra Chill site.
- Adding a show to [My Shows](https://events.extrachill.com/my-shows). Each show counts on the day you added it.

The calendar starts on **Last year**, which covers the past 12 months. Under the grid you see your total, your **Current streak** of active days in a row, and your **Longest** streak. Choose a year button to look back at any full year since you joined. Upvotes do not count toward the calendar.

## Keep your information current

Only add details and links you are comfortable sharing publicly. If a change does not appear, return to **Edit Profile**, make sure the information is saved, and check your public profile again. You need to be signed in to make changes.
