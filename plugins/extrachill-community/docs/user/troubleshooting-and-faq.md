# Troubleshooting and FAQs

Use these quick checks when something does not work as expected in the community.

## I cannot post, reply, or vote. What should I check?

Sign in first. Posting, replying, and voting require an account session. If you are signed in but the writing area or **Submit** option is missing, the forum or topic may be closed to new content. Check the notice on the page and try an open discussion.

## I cannot find my profile changes

Open your profile, choose **Edit Profile**, and save your changes. Then choose **View Public Profile** to check how it appears. Only information you choose to add appears for other members.

## Why did my points or rank not change right away?

Upvote changes are reflected directly in the total. Points from a new topic or reply may take a little time to appear. Check your profile or the leaderboard again later.

## Why did I not get a notification?

Notifications cover replies to topics you are subscribed to, mentions in forum posts, and new topics about festivals you subscribed to. They are not alerts for every post. Check the **Notifications** page and make sure you chose to hear about the relevant discussion or festival when that option was offered.

## Can I remove an upvote?

Yes. Choose the upvote control again to remove your vote. There is no downvote option.

## Where should I share a song or release?

Use **Music Discussion** for general music posts. Use **Local Scenes** when your post is about music or artists in your area. Add a listening link and enough context for others to join the conversation.

## What can other members see on my profile?

Your public profile may show your picture, bio, links, activity, and rank. Only add details and links you are comfortable sharing. See [Set up your profile](setting-up-your-user-profile.md).
