# Community ranks and points

Your rank is a quick way to see how much you have contributed around Extra Chill. You earn points by joining conversations, writing, and going to shows, and your rank grows with your total. You can find your rank and points on your profile and on the [community leaderboard](https://community.extrachill.com/all-users). Special role badges, when shown, are separate from your rank.

## How points add up

- Creating a forum topic earns 2 points.
- Replying earns 2 points.
- Each upvote you receive earns half a point.
- Each published article on the main Extra Chill site earns 10 points.
- Each show in [My Shows](https://events.extrachill.com/my-shows) earns 3 points once the show date has passed. Upcoming shows and ratings do not earn points.
- Each tour page or venue calendar page you submit that our team approves earns 10 points. It is 10 points per approved page, no matter how many shows it adds. See [Add your tour](https://docs.extrachill.com/events-calendar/add-your-tour).

Removing an upvote also removes its half point from the author's total. New points can take up to an hour to show up in your total.
