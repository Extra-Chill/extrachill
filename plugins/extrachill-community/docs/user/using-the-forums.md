# Use the forums

Find conversations about music and local scenes, or start one of your own. You can read topics before joining in.

## Start a topic

1. Sign in and open the forum that fits your subject, such as **Music Discussion** or **Live Shows & Scenes**.
2. Choose the option to create a topic.
3. Add a clear title and write your post in the writing area.
4. Add an image if it helps explain your post.
5. Choose **Submit**. If the post goes through, it appears in that forum.

When you start from a forum, your topic belongs there. If you start elsewhere, choose the right forum from the **Forum** list when it appears. Some choices may only be available when editing a topic.

## Reply to a topic

Open a discussion and use its reply area. Write your response and choose **Submit**. To mention someone, type **@** and their name where mentions are offered. They may receive a notification.

When you post or reply, an option to hear about later replies may appear. Choose it if you want updates for that topic. The wording can vary by screen.

## Your drafts are saved as you write

When you are signed in, a new topic or reply saves as a draft a moment after you stop typing. If you leave the page or close your browser, your words are waiting when you come back.

- **New topics:** open the new topic form again and your saved title, text, and forum choice come back, as long as the form is empty.
- **Replies:** return to the same topic and your saved reply comes back in the reply area. If you were replying to a specific post, that draft is kept separately.
- When you post the topic or reply, its draft is cleared.

## Tag artists, festivals, and places

Shortly after you post, Extra Chill may tag your topic with the artists, festivals, and places it talks about. Tagged topics can show up on the community pages for those artists, festivals, and places, so more people with the same interests find them.

To fix a tag, edit your topic and open **Correct classifications (optional)**. Search in **Location**, **Festival**, or **Artist**, pick from the list, or remove a tag you do not want. Then save your topic. You can only pick places, festivals, and artists that already exist on Extra Chill.

You can edit your own topics and replies for 30 minutes after posting.

## What to expect

You need to be signed in to post or reply. A closed forum or topic may not accept new content; read the message shown on the page. If the writing area or **Submit** option is missing, sign in and check whether the discussion is open. Share only content you have permission to post and that fits the community.

For recommendations and releases, see [Share music](sharing-music.md). You can also [upvote a post](upvoting-system.md) to show appreciation without replying.
