# Use the forums

Find conversations about music and local scenes, or start one of your own. You can read topics before joining in.

## Start a topic

1. Sign in and open the forum that fits your subject, such as **Music Discussion** or **Local Scenes**.
2. Choose the option to create a topic.
3. Add a clear title and write your post in the editor.
4. Add an image if it helps explain your post.
5. Submit the topic. It should appear in that forum if the submission succeeds.

When you start from a forum, the topic belongs there. If you start elsewhere, choose the right forum in the **Forum** list when that choice appears. Some classification options may appear only when editing an existing topic.

## Reply to a topic

Open a discussion and use its reply area. Write your response and submit it. To mention someone, type **@** and their name where mentions are offered. They may receive a notification.

You may also see an option to hear about later replies. Depending on the screen, it may describe email updates or notifications. The notification page shows activity in the community; email delivery depends on the choice shown to you.

## What to expect

- You must be signed in to post.
- A closed topic or forum may not accept new posts or replies. Follow the message shown on the page.
- Images and other content must be suitable for the community and yours to share.
- If the editor or submit option is missing, sign in and check whether the conversation is open.

For music recommendations or releases, see [Share music](sharing-music.md). You can also [upvote a post](upvoting-system.md) to show appreciation without replying.
