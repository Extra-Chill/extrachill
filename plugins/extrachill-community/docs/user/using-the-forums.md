# Use the forums

Find conversations about music and local scenes, or start one of your own. You can read topics before joining in.

## Start a topic

1. Sign in and open the forum that fits your subject, such as **Music Discussion** or **Local Scenes**.
2. Choose the option to create a topic.
3. Add a clear title and write your post in the writing area.
4. Add an image if it helps explain your post.
5. Choose **Submit**. If the post goes through, it appears in that forum.

When you start from a forum, your topic belongs there. If you start elsewhere, choose the right forum from the **Forum** list when it appears. Some choices may only be available when editing a topic.

## Reply to a topic

Open a discussion and use its reply area. Write your response and choose **Submit**. To mention someone, type **@** and their name where mentions are offered. They may receive a notification.

When you post or reply, an option to hear about later replies may appear. Choose it if you want updates for that topic. The wording can vary by screen.

## What to expect

You need to be signed in to post or reply. A closed forum or topic may not accept new content; read the message shown on the page. If the writing area or **Submit** option is missing, sign in and check whether the discussion is open. Share only content you have permission to post and that fits the community.

For recommendations and releases, see [Share music](sharing-music.md). You can also [upvote a post](upvoting-system.md) to show appreciation without replying.
