# Getting Started in the Extra Chill Community

Join music fans and artists for conversations, recommendations, and local music news. The community is a place to read discussions, share your own music, and take part at your own pace.

## Join and get help

Visit [Extra Chill Community](https://community.extrachill.com) to sign in or create an account. Use the same account when visiting other Extra Chill sites.

Keep posts respectful and on topic. Only share pictures and music you have permission to share, and avoid posting private information. If you need help, visit the [Tech Support Forum](https://community.extrachill.com/r/tech-support) or [contact Extra Chill](https://extrachill.com/contact/).

## Find a conversation

Browse the community homepage and choose a forum that fits what you want to discuss. Music Discussion is for music topics and recommendations. Local Scenes is for music and events in your area. Open a topic to read it and reply if you have something to add.

## Take part

Create a topic when you have something new to share, or reply to an existing one. You can add images to your post and mention another member by their @name. Upvote a topic or reply you appreciate. Members receive points for posting and for receiving upvotes.

## Make your profile yours

Add a picture, a short bio, your area, and links you want other members to see. Your profile also shows your community activity and rank.

You can return to the community to read recent discussions and activity. Notifications let you know when someone replies to a topic you follow, mentions you, or starts a topic about a festival you subscribe to.
