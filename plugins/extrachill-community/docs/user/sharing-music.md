# Sharing Your Music

Share a song, release, or recommendation with music fans and invite them into a conversation. Music Discussion is the main place for music posts. Local Scenes is a good fit when your post is about music or artists in your area.

1. Open the forum that fits your post.
2. Write a title that tells people what you are sharing.
3. Add a listening link and a little context, such as the style of music or what kind of feedback you want.
4. Add an image if you want to include artwork or a photo.
5. Submit your topic.

You can reply to people who join the discussion, and you may receive a notification when someone replies or mentions you. Upvote music posts and replies you enjoy. Sharing a post does not guarantee plays or feedback, so joining other conversations can help you meet people with similar interests.
