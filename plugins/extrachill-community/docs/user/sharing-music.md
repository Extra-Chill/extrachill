# Share music

Share a song, release, or recommendation and invite other fans into the conversation. **Music Discussion** is a good place for music posts. Choose **Local Scenes** when your post is about music or artists in your area.

## Start a music post

1. Sign in and open the forum that fits your post.
2. Start a topic with a clear title that tells readers what you are sharing.
3. Add a listening link and a little context, such as the style of music or the kind of feedback you would like.
4. Add an image if it helps show the release or artist.
5. Submit your topic. If it goes through, it appears in the forum you chose.

You can also open a discussion and reply when you have something to add. Upvote songs and replies you enjoy to show appreciation without writing a comment.

## What to expect

You need to be signed in to post. A closed forum or topic may not accept new posts or replies; check the message shown on the page. Only share music, images, and links you have permission to share. Posting does not promise plays or feedback, but joining other conversations can help you meet fans with similar interests.

If the writing area or submit option is missing, sign in and check whether the forum is open for posts. For help with replies and topics, see [Use the forums](using-the-forums.md).
