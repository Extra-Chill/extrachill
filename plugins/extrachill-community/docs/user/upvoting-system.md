# Upvote a post

An upvote is a quick way to show that a topic or reply was useful or enjoyable, without adding a comment.

Choose the upvote control on a topic or reply to vote for it. The number beside the control shows its votes. Choose the control again to remove your vote. There is no downvote option.

You need to be signed in to vote. Each upvote a member receives adds half a point to their community total; removing a vote removes that point value. These points can affect the member's rank. Creating a topic or reply also earns points. See [Community ranks](understanding-ranks.md) to learn more.

If you cannot use the control, sign in and reload the discussion. A closed topic may limit what you can do on that page.
