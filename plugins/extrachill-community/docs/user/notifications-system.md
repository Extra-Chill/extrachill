# Read and manage notifications

Notifications help you return to conversations that involve you. The bell shows your unread count, and the **Notifications** page brings recent activity together.

## Check your notifications

1. Sign in and choose the notification bell or open **Notifications** from the community navigation.
2. Review **New Notifications** first, then **Previously Viewed**.
3. Open an item to visit the related conversation. Opening a notification marks it as read; viewing the list alone does not.
4. Choose **Mark all as read** when you want to clear the unread count.

You may get a notification when someone replies to a topic you are subscribed to, or mentions you in a forum post. These are not alerts for every post. If you turned on the weekly Local Scene digest, you also get a weekly **Your Local Scene weekly picks are ready** notice. See [Notifications and email digests](https://docs.extrachill.com/accounts/notifications-and-digests).

## Hear about replies

When you post or reply, choose the topic update option if it is offered and you want to hear about later replies. To mention a member in a post or reply, type **@** and their name where mentions are offered.

If an expected item is missing, check whether you are signed in and whether you subscribed to the discussion. Notifications cover the activities above, not all community activity.
