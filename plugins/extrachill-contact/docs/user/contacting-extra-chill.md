# Contacting Extra Chill

Have a question, an idea, or a problem? The contact form sends your message straight to the Extra Chill team. Use it for things like account requests, partnerships, shop orders, or something that is not working right.

## Send a message

1. Open the [contact form](https://extrachill.com/contact-us).
2. Enter your **Name** and **Email**. Use the email address on your Extra Chill account if your message is about your account.
3. Pick a **Subject** that fits best: **General Inquiry**, **Partnership/Collaboration**, **Promoter or Collective Link Page**, **Venue Link Page**, **Shop/Store Support**, **Technical Issue**, or **Other**.
4. Write your **Message**. Include links, order details, or the page where you saw a problem so the team can help faster.
5. Send the form.

All four fields are required. A quick security check runs in the background before your message goes through.

## What happens next

When your message goes through, you see **Your message has been sent successfully. We'll get back to you soon.** You also get an email titled **Extra Chill Got Your Message** with a copy of what you wrote. The team aims to reply within 3–5 business days.

Sending the form also adds you to the Extra Chill newsletter; you can unsubscribe anytime. Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any newsletter email. See [Newsletter help](https://docs.extrachill.com/newsletter).

## Common requests

- **Account deletion:** see [Deleting your account](https://docs.extrachill.com/accounts/deleting-your-account).
- **A copy of your data:** send a request from the email address on your account. See [Account settings](https://docs.extrachill.com/accounts/account-settings).
- **Venue or promoter Link Pages:** choose **Venue Link Page** or **Promoter or Collective Link Page**. Tell us your venue's name and city, or who you are and the shows you run, and the team will set it up with you.

## If your message does not send

If you see **Your message could not be delivered. Please try again.** or **Security verification failed. Please try again.**, wait a moment and send it again. Make sure your email address is typed correctly. If you do not get the confirmation email, check your spam folder.
