# Review and manage booking inquiries

A timely, clear reply helps artists and promoters understand where their request stands.

## Open an inquiry

Use the booking inquiry area available to your venue to review requests. The inquiry page shown to the person who sent a request can display a status such as **Pending review** and a **Refresh status** button. The venue’s staff view depends on the access and pages available to your venue; if you cannot find it, ask the person who manages your venue’s site access.

Review the requested date and space, the sender’s contact details, and the information they provided. Compare the request with your venue’s current availability before you decide what to do.

## Respond to the request

Use your venue’s available booking controls to make and communicate a decision. Do not treat a submitted inquiry or an available date as a confirmed event. The sender’s status page shows the current status when they refresh it.

If you need a change or more information, use the response options available for the inquiry. Some changes may be sent as a request for review, rather than changing the original inquiry immediately. A cancellation request for a confirmed booking also asks the venue to review it; it does not cancel the booking at once.

## Keep track of updates

Check inquiries regularly and review the latest status before acting. A status can change while you are working. If a page says the inquiry changed, refresh it and review the updated details before trying again.

The sender can check their status with **Refresh status**. They may also have options to request a correction, withdraw an inquiry, or request cancellation, depending on the inquiry’s status. These options are not available for every inquiry.

## Access and permissions

You need the right access to the venue’s booking area to review and respond to requests. If booking controls are missing, your account may not have that access. Ask your venue’s site manager to check your access. Do not ask an artist to share their private inquiry access details.

## If something goes wrong

- **No inquiry appears:** Check that you are looking at the right venue and refresh the inquiry area. Ask the sender whether they saw the submission confirmation.
- **The sender cannot see their inquiry:** They can use **Recover your inquiry access** with the confirmation reference and private access code from their email. A signed-in sender may be able to use their account instead.
- **A status check fails:** Try **Refresh status** again. If the issue continues, note the inquiry details and ask the person who manages your venue’s site access for help.
- **A change does not go through:** Refresh the status first. The inquiry may have changed since you opened it, or that action may not be available for its current status.
