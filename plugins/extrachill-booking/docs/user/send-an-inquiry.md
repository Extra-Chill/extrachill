# Send a booking inquiry

A clear inquiry gives a venue the details it needs to consider your event request.

## Send your request

1. Open the venue’s booking inquiry form.
2. Fill in the details shown on the form. The questions can vary by venue.
3. Choose a requested date and space if those options are shown, and include a message with the details the venue asks for.
4. Select **Send booking inquiry**.

The form may ask you to confirm that you agree to the venue’s stated terms. Complete any required check shown before sending. If a question or check is missing, you may not be able to submit the form.

## What happens next

After you send the form, look for an on-screen confirmation and a confirmation email. The inquiry page says the email should arrive within a few minutes. Keep the email: it helps you return to the inquiry and see what happens next.

The venue reviews the inquiry. Sending a request does not mean the venue has confirmed a booking. The venue may need more information or may not be able to offer the requested date or space.

## If you cannot find or open the form

The form is shown for venues that accept booking inquiries. If it is not on the venue’s page, that venue may not have made the form available there. Check that you are on the intended venue’s page and try again later if the page does not load.

If you submitted the form but did not receive the email, check your spam folder and make sure you entered your email address correctly. Keep the confirmation page open while you check. If the inquiry page offers **Recover your inquiry access**, use the details requested there.

## Keep your request details handy

The inquiry confirmation page can show the status of your request and available next steps. Access to those details may depend on the confirmation information saved in your browser or the email sent after submission. Avoid sharing private access details with anyone else.
