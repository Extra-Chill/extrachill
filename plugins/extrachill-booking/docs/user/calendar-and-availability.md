# Set up your booking calendar and availability

A clear calendar helps artists ask about dates and spaces your venue can consider.

## Before you begin

You need access to your venue’s booking settings. The settings apply to a venue and its spaces. If you cannot see or change them, ask someone who manages the venue’s site access for help.

## Set up the calendar

1. Open your venue’s booking settings.
2. Turn on booking inquiries for the venue if they are not already on.
3. Add or check the spaces artists can request. Give each space a clear name and choose the default space if one is marked.
4. Review the questions and consent notice shown on the inquiry form. These can vary by venue.
5. Save your changes, then open the venue’s public page and check the inquiry form.

When the form is available, artists can choose from the spaces and provide the information your venue requests. A form may be unavailable if inquiries are turned off or its settings are not ready.

## What availability means

The form can check a requested time against venue availability. A time that overlaps a confirmed booking or a published event may be treated as filled. An available time is not a confirmed booking: the venue still needs to review and respond to the inquiry.

The form may ask for a date or time, depending on what is set up for the venue. Check the actual public form to see what artists can select. Do not promise a date until your venue has confirmed it.

## Keep your information current

Review the venue’s spaces and form questions when your offerings change. If a space should no longer receive requests, update the venue’s booking settings and check the public form afterward. A change to the form does not itself accept or confirm inquiries already sent.

## If the form or availability check is not working

- If the inquiry form is missing, check that inquiries are enabled and that the venue page is the correct one.
- If a space is missing, check that it is included in the venue’s current settings.
- If the form says it cannot check availability, try again later. If the problem continues, contact the person who manages the venue’s site access.
- If a date looks available but your venue cannot take the event, review the request before replying. A visible availability result does not replace your venue’s review.
