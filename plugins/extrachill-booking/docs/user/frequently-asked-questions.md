# Booking inquiry questions

## Is a date confirmed when I send an inquiry?

No. Sending a request asks the venue to review your event. A date shown as available is not a confirmed booking. Wait for the venue’s decision.

## Will every venue show the same form?

No. The venue chooses which spaces, questions, and consent information appear. Requested date and time options can also vary. Fill in the questions on the form you see.

## What should I do if the inquiry form is missing?

Make sure you are on the right venue page. A venue may not have booking inquiries available. If you manage the venue, check that inquiries are turned on and that the venue’s settings are ready.

## I sent an inquiry but cannot find it. What now?

Check your email, including the spam folder, for the confirmation. If the inquiry page offers **Recover your inquiry access**, enter the confirmation reference and private access code from the email. If you are signed in, the page may let your account authorize access instead.

## Can I change or cancel my request?

The inquiry page may offer **Correction for venue review** or **Withdraw inquiry**, depending on the current status. A correction sends a request to the venue; it does not silently change your inquiry. For a confirmed booking, **Request cancellation** asks the venue to review cancellation and does not cancel it immediately.

## Why can’t I see the venue’s booking controls?

Venue staff need the right access to their venue’s booking area. Ask the person who manages your venue’s site access if you cannot review or respond to inquiries.

## What if a time appears open but the venue cannot take my event?

The venue still reviews every inquiry. An availability result is not a promise that the date is approved. Contact the venue if you need to clarify a request.
