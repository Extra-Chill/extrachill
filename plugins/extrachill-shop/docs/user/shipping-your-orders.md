# Shipping Your Orders

You can print a paid shipping label for any order right from the shop manager, and the buyer's shipping charge pays for it. Or ship it your own way and keep the shipping money. Either way, buyers know what they're paying before they check out.

## What Buyers Pay for Shipping

- Every order pays a flat **$5** for shipping. One order is always from one shop, so it ships as one package.
- If every item in the order is marked **Ships free**, the buyer pays nothing for shipping.
- The shop only ships to addresses in the United States.

## Add Your Ship-From Address

Labels need to know where your package is coming from. Do this once before your first order.

1. Open the [shop manager](https://shop.extrachill.com/manage/) and go to the **Shipping** tab.
2. Fill in **Name (for shipping label)**, **Street Address**, **City**, **State**, and **ZIP Code**. Add an apartment or suite number if you have one.
3. Select **Save Address**.

Only US addresses are allowed.

## Option 1: Print a Label Through Extra Chill

1. Go to the **Orders** tab and open the order.
2. Check the package weight and box size, then select **Check Label Price**.
3. Select **Buy Label**.
4. Print the label, tape it to your package, and drop it at the post office or hand it to your mail carrier.

Extra Chill buys the cheapest mail service available for the package. The buyer's $5 shipping charge pays for it, so the label costs you nothing extra.

When the label is ready:

- The order is marked shipped and moves to **Completed**.
- The tracking number is saved on the order.
- We email you the label, the tracking number, and the ship-to address.
- You can select **Reprint Label** any time if you lose it.

**Package size:** before buying, enter your package's weight and box size. They're filled in from your products' weights and sizes, or a small package (1 pound, 10 × 8 × 4 inches) if your products don't have them. Select **Check Label Price** to see the label price next to the shipping the buyer paid. If the label costs more than the buyer's shipping, you can't buy it through Extra Chill: ship it yourself (option 2) and the shipping is paid out to you.

## Option 2: Ship It Yourself

1. Mail the order your own way, with any carrier.
2. Go to the **Orders** tab and open the order.
3. Enter the **Tracking Number** if you have one. It's optional, but it helps if a package goes missing.
4. Select **Mark as Shipped**.

Because you paid the postage, the buyer's $5 shipping charge is paid out to you when you mark the order shipped. After that, you can't buy a label for that order through Extra Chill. Buy one directly from the carrier instead.

## Ships Free Orders

If an order only has **Ships free** items, there's no label button. You'll see **Ships Free Order** instead. Mail it yourself, then select **Mark as Shipped**.

## Shipping Times

Ship within the time you tell buyers, or within 7 days if you haven't said. Your shop page doesn't have a separate spot for shipping times, so put yours in your product descriptions. Add tracking where you can. Buyers can see that their order is **Completed** once you've shipped it.
