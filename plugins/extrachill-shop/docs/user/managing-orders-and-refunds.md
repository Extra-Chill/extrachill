# Managing Orders and Refunds

Every order lands in one place, with the buyer's address, what they bought, and what you'll be paid. Ship it, refund it, or check on it from the **Orders** tab of the shop manager.

## When an Order Comes In

As soon as a buyer pays, we email everyone who manages your shop. For an artist, that's every member of the artist profile. For a venue or promoter, it's the owners. The email shows:

- The items and quantities.
- **Your Payout** for the order.
- The buyer's shipping charge, and what happens to it.
- The ship-to address.
- A **View Order in Shop Manager** button.

The **Orders** tab also shows a number for orders waiting to ship.

## Find an Order

Open the [shop manager](https://shop.extrachill.com/manage/) and go to the **Orders** tab. Pick a view:

- **All:** every paid order.
- **Needs Fulfillment:** paid orders you haven't shipped yet. Disputed orders are left out, because they mustn't ship.
- **Completed:** shipped and refunded orders.

Each order card shows the order number, its status, the buyer's name, the date, the number of items, and your payout. Select a card to open it.

## Inside an Order

An open order shows:

- **Customer:** the buyer's name and email.
- **Shipping Address:** where to send it.
- **Items:** each product, quantity, and total.
- **Your Payout:** what reaches your payout account for this order. See [Fees, Payouts, and Taxes](fees-payouts-and-taxes.md).
- **Tracking:** once you've added it.

Select **Back** to return to the list.

## Order Status

- **processing:** paid and waiting for you to ship.
- **on-hold:** paused.
- **disputed:** the buyer disputed the payment with their bank (see below).
- **completed:** shipped.
- **refunded:** the buyer got their money back.

## Ship an Order

Open the order, then buy a label with **Check Label Price** and **Buy Label**, or ship it yourself and select **Mark as Shipped**. Either way, the order moves to **completed** and the buyer gets an email saying their order is complete. See [Shipping Your Orders](shipping-your-orders.md).

The buyer's email doesn't include tracking, so if they ask, send them the tracking number from the order.

## Refund an Order

1. Open the order.
2. Select **Refund Order**.
3. The **Refund amount** starts at everything still refundable on the order. Leave it for a full refund, or lower it for a partial refund, like one missing item.
4. Select **Yes, Refund**.

The money goes back the way the buyer paid. A full refund includes shipping and changes the order to **refunded**. After a partial refund, the order shows **Refunded so far**, and you can refund more later, up to what's left.

Extra Chill's fee is returned to you in proportion. Refund $10 of a $25 order and you get back $10/$25 of the fee. On a full refund the buyer's shipping charge comes back to you too, unless it already paid for an Extra Chill label. In that case the postage was spent, so that part stays with Extra Chill. Card processing on the original payment doesn't come back on any refund. See [Fees, Payouts, and Taxes](fees-payouts-and-taxes.md).

Refund from the shop manager when you can. If you refund from your payout dashboard instead, we record the refund on the order, add a note, and return the same share of Extra Chill's fee.

You can refund an order before or after you ship it. Under the [Seller Agreement](https://extrachill.com/seller-agreement/), you replace or refund anything that arrives damaged, defective, or very different from your listing.

## If a Buyer Disputes a Payment

Sometimes a buyer asks their bank to reverse a charge. When that happens:

- The order goes on hold and shows as **disputed**, with a warning at the top.
- We email your shop's managers with the amount and the reason.
- **Mark as Shipped**, **Buy Label**, and **Refund Order** are turned off for that order, and it leaves **Needs Fulfillment**. If you haven't shipped yet, don't.

Respond from your payout dashboard (the button is on the **Payments** tab), under payments and disputes, before the deadline shown there. If you don't respond in time, the bank usually sides with the buyer. Disputed amounts and dispute fees come out of your balance.

When the bank decides, we update the order and email your shop's managers:

- **You win:** you keep the payment. The hold comes off and the order goes back to the status it had before the dispute. If you haven't shipped it, it's back in **Needs Fulfillment**, and you can ship, buy a label, or refund it again.
- **The buyer wins:** the bank gives the disputed amount back to the buyer. We record it as a refund on the order and add a note, so nothing is refunded twice. If the whole order went back, it changes to **refunded**. Don't ship it. Extra Chill's fee isn't returned when a dispute is lost.

## Buyer Questions and Privacy

Answer buyer questions about their orders within 3 business days. Use the buyer's name, address, and email only to fill and support their order. Don't add them to a mailing list unless they signed up for it themselves.
