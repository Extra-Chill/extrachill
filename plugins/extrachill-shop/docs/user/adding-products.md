# Adding Products

Every product you add shows up on your shop page and in the Extra Chill Shop, so fans can buy it any time. A good product has a clear photo, a fair price, and accurate sizes and stock.

## Before You Start

Your payout account has to be ready before a product can go on sale. You can still build products as drafts while you finish setup. See [Connecting Your Payout Account](connecting-your-payout-account.md).

## Add a Product

1. Open the [shop manager](https://shop.extrachill.com/manage/) and go to the **Products** tab.
2. Select **Add Product**, or **Create your first product** if your shop is empty.
3. Enter a **Name**.
4. Select **Choose Images** and add up to 5 photos. The first one is the main photo buyers see. Drag them to change the order.
5. Enter a **Price**. Add a **Sale Price** if you want to mark it down.
6. Write a **Description**: what it is, what it's made of, and how it fits.
7. Set your stock and sizes (see below).
8. Check **Ships free** if you'll mail it yourself without a label, like a sticker or a patch.
9. Choose **Published** to put it on sale, or **Draft** to keep working on it.
10. Select **Create Product**.

To publish, a product needs a name, a price above zero, at least one photo, and a payout account that can receive payments.

## Stock and Sizes

- **No sizes:** enter a **Stock Quantity**. Leave it blank for unlimited.
- **Sizes:** check **This product has sizes**, then enter how many you have of each size from XS to XXL. Leave a size at 0 if you don't carry it. Buyers can only pick sizes you have in stock.

Stock goes down on its own as orders come in. When something sells out, buyers can't add it to their cart.

## Shipping Options

Most products ship with a flat $5 shipping charge per order, which covers a shipping label. Check **Ships free** only for small, light items you'll mail yourself. If every item in an order ships free, the buyer pays no shipping. See [Shipping Your Orders](shipping-your-orders.md).

## Edit, Hide, or Delete a Product

- Select the pencil icon to edit a product. Make your changes and select **Save Changes**.
- To take a product off sale without losing it, edit it and switch it to **Draft**.
- Select the trash icon to delete a product.

Each product shows its price and a label with its status, like **publish** or **draft**.

## Product Review

Our team may review new products, and we can take down any product that breaks the [Seller Agreement](https://extrachill.com/seller-agreement/). You can only sell things you own or have the right to sell, and your listings must be accurate.

Some things can't be sold at all, including anything illegal, counterfeit, stolen, or dangerous, adult-only items, weapons, drugs, alcohol, tobacco and vape products, and resold tickets. The Seller Agreement has the full list.

## You're Responsible for Your Products

You make, stock, ship, and stand behind everything you sell. Extra Chill never holds or ships your stock. Keep your stock numbers up to date so buyers don't order something you've run out of.
