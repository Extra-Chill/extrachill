# Your Orders, Shipping, and Returns

After you check out, here's how to keep track of your order and get help if something goes wrong.

## Who Handles Your Order

Every order on the shop comes from one seller: an artist, venue, promoter, or Extra Chill itself. That seller packs and ships your order and handles returns, refunds, and questions about it. Extra Chill runs the shop, but each seller is responsible for its own orders and support. If you can't sort it out with the seller, contact us through the [contact form](https://extrachill.com/contact-us/) and choose **Shop/Store Support**.

## Order Emails

You'll get an email confirming your order once it's paid. You'll get another when your order is complete. For merch, that means the seller has shipped it.

Lifetime memberships and event boosts complete right away, so both emails arrive close together.

If you checked out without an account, keep these emails. They're your order record.

## See Your Orders

1. Log in to Extra Chill.
2. Open [My account](https://shop.extrachill.com/my-account/) on the shop.
3. Select **Orders** to see each order, what's in it, and where it stands.

Orders placed while you were logged out don't show up here. Ticket purchases don't either. Your pass emails are your record for those.

## Order Status

- **Pending payment:** you started checkout but haven't paid yet.
- **Processing:** your payment went through and the seller is getting your order ready.
- **On hold:** the order is paused, usually while a payment question is sorted out.
- **Completed:** the seller shipped your order, or for memberships and boosts, it's active.
- **Cancelled:** the order was never paid, usually because the payment page timed out.
- **Failed:** the payment didn't go through.
- **Refunded:** your money was returned.

## When Your Order Ships

Sellers ship within the time they list, usually in the product description, or within 7 days if they don't list one. The order changes to **Completed** when it ships.

If you'd like the tracking number, ask the seller. They have it once they've shipped.

## Returns and Refunds

Each seller sets its own shipping times and return policy. Look for **Shipping & returns** on the seller's storefront or the **Shipping & Returns** tab on its product pages. Every seller agrees to replace or refund items that arrive damaged, defective, or very different from the listing. Contact the seller within 30 days of delivery and include photos.

Refunds go back to the way you paid. Once a seller refunds your order, its status changes to **Refunded** and you get an email.

Read the full [Shipping & Returns Policy](https://shop.extrachill.com/shipping-and-returns/) for details, including lost packages and tickets.

## Lost Packages

If tracking says your package was delivered but you don't have it, check with neighbors and your local post office first, then contact the seller. The seller looks into packages that never show as delivered.

## Get Help

Contact the seller first. If you can't reach them or can't sort it out, use the [contact form](https://extrachill.com/contact-us/) and choose **Shop/Store Support**. Include your order number, and we'll help where we can.
