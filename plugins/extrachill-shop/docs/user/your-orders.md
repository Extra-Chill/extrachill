# Your Orders, Shipping, and Returns

After you check out, here's how to keep track of your order and get help if something goes wrong.

## Order Emails

You'll get an email confirming your order once it's paid. You'll get another when your order is complete. For merch, that means the seller has shipped it.

Lifetime memberships and event boosts complete right away, so both emails arrive close together.

If you checked out without an account, keep these emails. They're your order record.

## See Your Orders

1. Log in to Extra Chill.
2. Open [My account](https://shop.extrachill.com/my-account/) on the shop.
3. Select **Orders** to see each order, what's in it, and where it stands.

Orders placed while you were logged out don't show up here.

## Order Status

- **Processing:** your payment went through and the seller is getting your items ready.
- **Completed:** your order shipped, or for memberships and boosts, it's active.
- **Refunded:** some or all of your money was returned.

## Orders With Several Artists

Each artist ships their own items, so one order can arrive in more than one package. Shipping was charged once per artist at checkout for the same reason.

## Returns, Refunds, and Lost Packages

Read the [Shipping & Returns Policy](https://shop.extrachill.com/shipping-and-returns/) for what can be returned and what to do if a package goes missing.

To ask about an order, use the [contact form](https://extrachill.com/contact-us/) and choose **Shop/Store Support**. Include your order number. If you get a refund, it goes back to the way you paid.
