# Fees, Payouts, and Taxes

Your current charges are shown before you sell, so you can see what comes out of each sale. Your payout account receives the payment, and then sends money to your bank on its schedule.

## What Extra Chill Charges

- **Merch:** a percentage of the item price after discounts, not including shipping. The current rate appears on the **Payments** tab and under **Price** when you add a product.
- **Tickets:** the current seller fee and any buyer service fee appear on the **Payments** tab before you sell. Check there for the current rates.
- **Opening a shop and listing products:** no charge. The buyer's shipping charge covers a label when you buy one through the shop.

The fee is taken automatically when the buyer pays. You don't get a separate bill. Your current charges are shown before you sell.

## Payment Processing Fees

Our payment partner charges a processing fee on every card payment. It comes out of your payout account under their pricing, not out of Extra Chill's fee. You can see it on each payment in your payout dashboard.

## What Happens to the Buyer's Shipping

Buyers pay a flat $5 shipping charge on most orders. When the buyer pays, Extra Chill sets it aside.

- **You print the label through Extra Chill:** the $5 pays for your label, so you pay nothing for postage.
- **You ship it yourself and select Mark as Shipped:** the $5 goes back to you, since you paid the postage.

See [Shipping Your Orders](shipping-your-orders.md).

## An Example

For example, if the item price is $25 and shipping is $5, the buyer pays $30 before any tax that may apply. The current merch fee is based on the item price after discounts, not shipping. Your actual payout is shown on the order and can also be affected by payment processing fees and refunds.

When you buy a label through the shop, the buyer's shipping charge goes toward the label. If you ship it yourself and mark the order shipped, the shipping charge is paid out to you.

## How Payouts Work

- Every sale is paid straight into your payout account. Extra Chill never holds your money.
- Money moves from your payout account to your bank on its regular schedule.
- Select **Open payout dashboard** on the **Payments** tab of the [shop manager](https://shop.extrachill.com/manage/) to see your balance, each payment, and upcoming payouts.

## Refunds and Disputes

When you refund an order, Extra Chill's fee on it is returned to you. A partial refund returns the same share of the fee: refund $10 of a $25 order and $10/$25 of the fee comes back. This also applies to refunds you make from your payout dashboard. Payment processing fees on the original sale may not come back.

If a buyer disputes a payment with their bank, the disputed amount and any dispute fee come out of your balance. See [Managing Orders and Refunds](managing-orders-and-refunds.md).

## Taxes

You are responsible for your own taxes. That includes working out, collecting, reporting, and paying any sales, use, or admissions tax on your sales, plus income tax on what you earn.

- No sales tax is added at checkout. Treat the prices you set as including tax.
- Ticket prices include tax too. Venues and promoters handle any admissions tax.
- You may get a tax form for your sales where the law requires one. Keep your tax details up to date in your payout account when asked.

If the law ever requires Extra Chill to collect tax on your sales, we'll tell you in advance.

## Your Products, Your Responsibility

You're the seller on every sale. You're responsible for what you list, for shipping it, and for standing behind it. Read the [Seller Agreement](https://extrachill.com/seller-agreement/) for the full terms.
