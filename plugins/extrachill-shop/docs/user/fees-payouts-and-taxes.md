# Fees, Payouts, and Taxes

Selling on Extra Chill works like a merch table at a festival. We bring the booth, the checkout, and the crowd. You bring the goods and keep the money, minus a small fee and card processing on merch. On tickets you keep the whole ticket price. Here's exactly what comes out of each sale and when you get paid.

## What Extra Chill Charges

- **Merch:** 10% of the price of the items in each order, after any discount, plus card processing on the whole payment. There's no 10% fee on shipping.
- **Tickets:** nothing. You keep the full ticket price. Buyers pay a service fee of $1 per ticket plus the card processing cost, shown as one **Service fee** line before they pay.
- **Opening a shop, listing products, and printing labels:** free.

The fee is taken automatically when the buyer pays. You never get a bill. The current rates are always shown on the **Payments** tab of the shop manager, and the merch rate is shown under **Price** when you add a product. The rates are set for Extra Chill as a whole, not separately by each shop. If fees ever go up, we'll give you at least 30 days' notice, and the new rate only applies to sales after it starts.

## Payment Processing Fees

Every card payment has a processing fee of about 2.9% plus 30¢, worked out on the whole amount the buyer pays.

- **Merch:** you pay it, on the whole payment, shipping included. It's taken when the buyer pays, at the same time as Extra Chill's fee, so you never get a bill. Depending on how your payout account is set up, your payout dashboard shows it either as part of Extra Chill's fee or as its own processing fee. Either way, you get the same amount.
- **Tickets:** the buyer's service fee covers it, so you end up with the full ticket price.

Processing is worked out at the usual card rate. A few cards, like ones from outside the US, cost more to process. Depending on how your payout account is set up, that extra cost can come out of your sale, so you may get a few cents less on merch or end up a few cents short of the full ticket price.

## What Happens to the Buyer's Shipping

Buyers pay a flat $5 shipping charge on most orders. When the buyer pays, Extra Chill sets it aside.

- **You print the label through Extra Chill:** the $5 pays for your label, so you pay nothing for postage.
- **You ship it yourself and select Mark as Shipped:** the $5 goes back to you, since you paid the postage.

See [Shipping Your Orders](shipping-your-orders.md).

## An Example

A fan buys a $25 shirt and pays $5 shipping. **The buyer pays $30.**

- Extra Chill's fee is 10% of $25: **$2.50**.
- Card processing on the $30 payment is about **$1.17**.
- The $5 shipping is set aside.
- **You get $21.33.**

If you print the label through Extra Chill, you keep $21.33 and the label is paid for. If you ship it yourself and mark it shipped, the $5 comes back to you and you get **$26.33**.

A few more orders:

| Order | Buyer pays | Extra Chill's 10% | Card processing | You get (label printed through Extra Chill) | You get (you ship it yourself) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $25 shirt, $5 shipping | $30.00 | $2.50 | $1.17 | $21.33 | $26.33 |
| $10 item, $5 shipping | $15.00 | $1.00 | $0.74 | $8.26 | $13.26 |
| $3 sticker, free shipping | $3.00 | $0.30 | $0.39 | $2.31 | $2.31 |

Processing can be a cent different from these amounts, and more on pricier cards.

## A Ticket Example

A fan buys one $20 ticket.

- The buyer pays $20 plus a **$1.94** service fee: **$21.94**.
- Card processing on $21.94 is $0.94, and Extra Chill keeps $1.
- **You get $20**, the full ticket price.

Four $20 tickets: the buyer pays $80 plus a **$6.82** service fee, $86.82 in total. Processing is $2.82, Extra Chill keeps $4, and **you get $80**.

## How Payouts Work

- Every sale is paid straight into your payout account. Extra Chill never holds your money.
- Money moves from your payout account to your bank on its regular schedule.
- Select **Open payout dashboard** on the **Payments** tab of the [shop manager](https://shop.extrachill.com/manage/) to see your balance, each payment, and upcoming payouts.

## Refunds and Disputes

When you refund an order, Extra Chill's 10% fee on it is returned to you. A partial refund returns the same share of the fee: refund $10 of a $25 order and $10/$25 of the fee comes back. This also applies to refunds you make from your payout dashboard. Card processing on the original payment doesn't come back. It was spent when the buyer paid, and refunding the buyer doesn't return it.

Say you fully refund the $30 shirt order above before you print a label. The buyer gets $30 back. The $2.50 fee and the $5 shipping come back to you, and the $1.17 processing doesn't.

When you refund a ticket order, the buyer gets back everything they paid, service fee included, and Extra Chill's share comes back to you. Card processing on the original payment may not come back.

If a buyer disputes a payment with their bank, the disputed amount and any dispute fee come out of your balance. See [Managing Orders and Refunds](managing-orders-and-refunds.md).

## Taxes

You are responsible for your own taxes. That includes working out, collecting, reporting, and paying any sales, use, or admissions tax on your sales, plus income tax on what you earn.

- No sales tax is added at checkout. Treat the prices you set as including tax.
- Ticket prices include tax too. Venues and promoters handle any admissions tax.
- You may get a tax form for your sales where the law requires one. Keep your tax details up to date in your payout account when asked.

If the law ever requires Extra Chill to collect tax on your sales, we'll tell you in advance.

## Your Products, Your Responsibility

You're the seller on every sale. You're responsible for what you list, for shipping it, and for standing behind it. Read the [Seller Agreement](https://extrachill.com/seller-agreement/) for the full terms.
