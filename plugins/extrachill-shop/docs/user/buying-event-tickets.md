# Buying Event Tickets

Some shows on the [Extra Chill Events Calendar](https://events.extrachill.com/) sell tickets right on the event page. You pay the venue or promoter directly, and your passes arrive by email, ready to scan at the door.

## Buy Tickets

1. Open the show on the Events Calendar.
2. Select **Buy tickets**. You'll see the price and, if the show has a limit, how many tickets are left.
3. Choose a **Quantity**. You can buy up to 10 tickets in one order.
4. Enter your **Name** and **Email**. The name goes on your passes, and the passes go to that email.
5. Agree to the [Terms of Service](https://extrachill.com/terms-of-service/), then choose **Buy tickets** to go to the secure payment page.
6. Check the total, including any service fee shown there, and pay.

The payment page stays open for 30 minutes. If you close it or run out of time, nothing is charged and you can start over from the event page.

You don't need an Extra Chill account to buy tickets.

## Get Your Passes

When your payment goes through, you come back to the event page and see "Your tickets are on the way." You get one email for each ticket, and each one has its own pass.

Show the pass on your phone at the door. Staff scan it to check you in. Each pass works once.

Ticket purchases don't show up under **My account** on the shop. Keep your pass emails.

## If a Show Sells Out While You Pay

If the last tickets sell while you're on the payment page, you're refunded in full automatically. You won't get passes for that order.

## Refunds

Tickets are sold by the show's venue or promoter, and they handle refunds.

- They can refund your order any time **before** anyone on it checks in at the door.
- Once a pass on your order is checked in, the order can't be refunded through Extra Chill.
- If a show is cancelled, the seller refunds every ticket holder.

Refunds go back to the way you paid. To ask for one, contact the venue or promoter. See the [Shipping & Returns Policy](https://shop.extrachill.com/shipping-and-returns/) for the full ticket rules.

## Sold Out or No Button?

If the page says **Sold out**, there are no tickets left. If there's no **Buy tickets** button at all, the show isn't selling tickets on Extra Chill. It may link to another ticket seller instead.
