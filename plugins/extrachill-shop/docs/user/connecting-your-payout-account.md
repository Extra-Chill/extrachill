# Connecting Your Payout Account

Your payout account is where sales money is received and sent to your bank. You need one before products can go on sale. Each person who manages the shop must accept the [Seller Agreement](https://extrachill.com/seller-agreement/) before setting up payouts.

## Connect Your Account

1. Open the [shop manager](https://shop.extrachill.com/manage/) and choose the shop you're setting up.
2. Go to the **Payments** tab.
3. Read the [Seller Agreement](https://extrachill.com/seller-agreement/), then check **I agree to the Seller Agreement**.
4. Select **Connect payouts**. You'll go to a secure setup page run by our payment partner.
5. Enter your details: who you are, your business details if you have them, and the bank account you want paid into.
6. When you finish, you come back to the shop manager.

You must be at least 18 to sell. If you set up payouts for a band, venue, or business, you're confirming you're allowed to act for it. Complete all requested identity, business, and bank details on the secure setup page. If more information is needed or your details are being checked, return to the Payments tab and follow its prompt.

If you leave partway through, come back to the **Payments** tab and select **Connect payouts** again to pick up where you left off.

## Check Your Status

The **Payments** tab shows where your account stands:

- **Charges enabled:** buyers can pay you.
- **Payouts enabled:** money can move to your bank.
- **Details submitted:** you finished the setup form.
- **Can receive payments:** everything is ready. Your products can go on sale.

Select **Refresh Status** if you just finished setup and the tab hasn't caught up.

If the status says **pending**, you started setup but haven't finished it. If it says **restricted**, you sent your details but more information is needed, or they're still being checked. Either way, select the button on the **Payments** tab to finish. When everything is ready, the status says **active**.

Until your account can receive payments, you can't publish products and buyers can't check out with you.

## See Your Money

Once you're connected, select **Open payout dashboard** on the **Payments** tab. It opens your payout dashboard in a new tab, where you can see your balance, each payment, upcoming payouts to your bank, and your bank details.

Money moves to your bank on your payout account's schedule. See [Fees, Payouts, and Taxes](fees-payouts-and-taxes.md) for what Extra Chill takes from each sale.

## Venues and Promoters

Your shop's payout account also receives your ticket sales. Ticket prices are set on each show, and ticket payouts and refunds show in the same dashboard. See [Selling Event Tickets](selling-event-tickets.md).

## Several People, One Shop

Everyone who can manage a shop sees the same payout account. Each person who connects or reconnects it agrees to the Seller Agreement for themselves first.
