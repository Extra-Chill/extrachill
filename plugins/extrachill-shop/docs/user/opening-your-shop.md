# Opening Your Shop

If you're an artist, venue, or promoter on Extra Chill, you can open a shop and sell to buyers across Extra Chill. Buyers shop from your own page, the money goes straight to you, and you can print shipping labels without leaving Extra Chill.

## Who Can Open a Shop

- **Artists:** anyone listed as a member on a published artist profile.
- **Venues:** venue owners.
- **Promoters:** organization owners.

If you manage more than one of these, each one gets its own shop.

## Open the Shop Manager

1. Log in to Extra Chill.
2. Open your account menu and select **Create Shop**. If you already have products, or you're a venue or promoter owner, it says **Manage Shop**.

You can also go straight to the [shop manager](https://shop.extrachill.com/manage/). If it says shop management isn't available for your account, you're not a member of an artist profile, or an owner of a venue or promoter, yet.

## Find Your Way Around

The shop manager shows the name of the shop you're working on at the top. It has four tabs:

- **Products:** add and edit what you sell. See [Adding Products](adding-products.md).
- **Orders:** see new orders, ship them, and refund them. A number on the tab shows how many orders need shipping. See [Managing Orders and Refunds](managing-orders-and-refunds.md).
- **Shipping:** the address your orders ship from. See [Shipping Your Orders](shipping-your-orders.md).
- **Payments:** your payout account. See [Connecting Your Payout Account](connecting-your-payout-account.md).

If you manage more than one shop, use the **Select shop** menu at the top to switch. **View Shop** opens your shop page the way buyers see it.

## Set Up in Four Steps

1. **Agree to the Seller Agreement and connect your payout account** on the **Payments** tab. Nothing goes on sale until this is done.
2. **Add your shipping address** on the **Shipping** tab.
3. **Add your first product** on the **Products** tab.
4. **Share your shop page.**

## The Seller Agreement

Before you can get paid, you agree to the [Seller Agreement](https://extrachill.com/seller-agreement/). Read it first. It covers what you're responsible for, what Extra Chill charges, and how refunds and disputes work. The short version:

- You're the seller. Every sale is your sale to your customer.
- You're responsible for your products: making them, stocking them, shipping them, and standing behind them.
- You're responsible for your own taxes.
- You tell buyers your shipping time and return policy, ship within that time (or 7 days), and answer buyer questions within 3 business days. Your shop page doesn't have a separate spot for policies, so put them in your product descriptions.

Each person who sets up payouts for a shop agrees for themselves. When the agreement changes in a big way, you'll be asked to agree to the new version.

## Your Shop Page

Every shop has its own page, like shop.extrachill.com/artist/your-name/. It shows all your published products, with a link back to your artist profile or upcoming shows. Your products also appear on the shop's front page, where buyers can pick your shop from **All Sellers**.

When you're logged in, your shop page has a **Manage Shop** button that takes you back to the shop manager.

## Stop Selling

You can stop at any time. Set your products to **Draft** or delete them, and ship or refund any open orders first.
