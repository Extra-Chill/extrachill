# Checking Out and Paying

Checkout takes a couple of minutes. You enter where your order should go, agree to the shop's terms, and pay on a secure payment page. Your card details go to the payment page, not to the seller.

## Before You Start

- **One seller per order.** Your cart can only hold items from one seller. See [Shopping the Extra Chill Shop](shopping-the-extra-chill-shop.md).
- **United States addresses only.** The shop doesn't ship outside the US yet. Checkout won't go through with an address in another country.
- **No account needed.** You can check out as a guest. If you're logged in, your order is saved under **My account**.

## Check Out

1. Open your cart and select **Proceed to checkout**.
2. Enter your email address and billing and shipping details. Double-check the shipping address; the seller sends the order there.
3. Check your total. Shipping is $5 per order, or free when every item ships free. Orders ship to US addresses only.
4. Check the box to agree to the [Terms of Service](https://extrachill.com/terms-of-service/). You must agree before placing the order.
5. Select **Pay securely** and place your order. You then go to a secure payment page to pay.

## Pay on the Secure Payment Page

After you place your order, you go to a secure payment page that shows each item and your shipping. Pay there.

When your payment goes through, you come back to an order confirmation page and get a confirmation email. See [Your Orders, Shipping, and Returns](your-orders.md) for what happens next.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **You changed your mind on the payment page.** Go back, and you'll land on your cart. Nothing is charged.
- **You waited too long.** The payment page stays open for about an hour. After that, your unpaid order is cancelled. Start again from your cart.
- **You see "Seller cannot receive payments."** The seller's payout account isn't ready, so they can't take orders right now. Try again later.
- **You see "This order has already been paid."** Your payment already went through. Check your email for the receipt instead of paying again.
- **Your payment failed later.** Some payment methods, like bank payments, take a few days to clear. If one fails, your order is marked **Failed** and won't ship.

## Taxes

No sales tax is added at checkout. The price you see is the price you pay, plus shipping.
