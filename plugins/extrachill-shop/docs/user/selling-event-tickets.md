# Selling Event Tickets

Venues and promoters can sell tickets right on a show's page on the [Extra Chill Events Calendar](https://events.extrachill.com/). Fans buy in a few taps, the money goes straight to your payout account, and every ticket comes with a pass that scans at your door.

## Who Can Sell Tickets

- **Venue owners**, for their venue.
- **Promoter organization owners**, for their organization.

Artists can't sell tickets through Extra Chill. Sell merch in your shop instead. See [Opening Your Shop](opening-your-shop.md).

## Before You Start

The venue or promoter selling the tickets needs a payout account that can receive payments. Set it up on the **Payments** tab of the [shop manager](https://shop.extrachill.com/manage/). See [Connecting Your Payout Account](connecting-your-payout-account.md).

Tickets work alongside the show's guest list, and every ticket is a guest list pass. If you haven't run a guest list before, read [Hosting a Guest List](https://docs.extrachill.com/events-calendar/hosting-a-guest-list/).

## Put Tickets on Sale

1. Log in and open the show's page on the Events Calendar.
2. Scroll to **Manage guest list**.
3. Under **Paid tickets**, check **Enable tickets**.
4. Enter the **Price per ticket**. This is the full price buyers pay, with any tax included.
5. Enter a **Quantity**. Enter 0 to use the guest list's spots as your limit.
6. Choose the **Seller**: the venue or promoter that gets paid.
7. Select **Save and enable guest list**.

If you see "This seller has to finish payment setup before tickets can go on sale," finish your payout account first.

Once tickets are on, the show's page has a **Buy tickets** button in place of any outside ticket link.

## What Buyers Get

Buyers choose up to 10 tickets, enter a name and email, and pay on a secure payment page. Each ticket arrives by email as its own pass. Ticket buyers count toward your guest list spots, and they show up on your door list. Scan their passes at the door like any guest list pass. See [Working the Door](https://docs.extrachill.com/events-calendar/working-the-door/).

When tickets run out, the page shows **Sold out**. If the last tickets sell while someone is paying, that buyer is refunded automatically.

## See Your Sales

Owners can see ticket sales in their venue or promoter workspace on the Events Calendar, under **Ticket sales**. Each show lists its orders with the buyer, number of tickets, when they bought, and the status: **Paid**, **Checked in**, or **Refunded**. Select **Guest list and ticket settings** to jump back to a show's ticket settings.

Ticket payments and payouts also show in your payout dashboard.

## Refund Tickets

- **One order:** select **Refund** next to it. You can refund any order until someone on it checks in at the door. After that, it can't be refunded through Extra Chill.
- **Cancelled show:** select **Show cancelled? Refund everyone**. This refunds every order that hasn't checked in and turns ticket sales off.

Refunded passes stop working at the door. Extra Chill's fee on a refunded order is returned to you. If you cancel a show, the [Seller Agreement](https://extrachill.com/seller-agreement/) asks you to refund every ticket holder.

### If a Buyer Disputes a Ticket Payment

If a buyer disputes a ticket payment with their bank, their passes stop working, the order shows as **Refunded** under **Ticket sales** (unless someone on it already checked in), and you can't refund it from Extra Chill. We email you when it happens. Respond from your payout dashboard before the deadline shown there. If they already checked in, say so in your response.

When the bank decides, we email you again:

- **You win:** you keep the payment. The buyer's passes work again, with the same codes, and we email them to the buyer. The order shows as **Paid** again. You can refund the order again until someone on it checks in.
- **The buyer wins:** the bank gives the money back to the buyer. Their passes stay cancelled, the order still can't be refunded, and it stays **Refunded** under **Ticket sales**. Extra Chill's fee isn't returned when a dispute is lost.

## Fees and Taxes

Right now Extra Chill takes no fee on ticket sales and adds no service fee for buyers. The current rates are always shown on the **Payments** tab of the shop manager. See [Fees, Payouts, and Taxes](fees-payouts-and-taxes.md).

You set the full price, with tax included. You're responsible for any sales or admissions tax on your tickets.
