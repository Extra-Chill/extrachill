# Shopping the Extra Chill Shop

The [Extra Chill Shop](https://shop.extrachill.com/) is where you buy Extra Chill merch, support artists by buying their merch, and pick up extras for your account or your events.

The shop is still growing. While it's being built, you'll see a notice at the top of the page and a small selection.

## What You Can Buy

- **Extra Chill merch**, like stickers.
- **Artist merch** from artists who sell through Extra Chill.
- **Lifetime Extra Chill Membership.** A one-time purchase that removes ads across every Extra Chill site.
- **Event Priority Boost.** Puts an upcoming event first on the calendar for its date.

## Place an Order

1. Open a product and select **Add to cart**.
2. Open your cart from the cart icon. Change quantities, apply a coupon if you have one, or remove items.
3. Go to checkout and enter your billing and shipping details.
4. Check your total, including shipping, and place your order.

You can check out without an Extra Chill account. If you're logged in, the order is saved to your account. See [Your Orders, Shipping, and Returns](your-orders.md).

## Buying a Lifetime Membership

When you add the membership to your cart, enter the **Community Username** of the account that should get it. If you're logged in, your own username is filled in for you. You can change it to give the membership to someone else.

The username must belong to an existing Extra Chill account. Once your payment goes through, that account stops seeing ads on every Extra Chill site.

## Boosting an Event

When you add Event Priority Boost to your cart, paste the **Event URL** of the event you want to boost. It has to be an event page on the [Extra Chill Events Calendar](https://events.extrachill.com/), and the event date can't be in the past.

Once your payment goes through, the event moves to the top of the calendar for its date.

## Shipping

Shipping is added at checkout before you pay. It's charged once per artist in your cart, so several items from the same artist ship together. If every item in your cart ships free, so does your order.

Memberships and event boosts don't ship. There's nothing to wait for.

## Selling Your Own Merch?

Artists set up their own store from their artist profile. Read [Creating Your Artist Merch Store](https://docs.extrachill.com/artist-platform/creating-your-artist-merch-store/).
