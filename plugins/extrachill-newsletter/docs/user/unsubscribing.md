# Unsubscribing from the Newsletter

You can stop the Extra Chill newsletter at any time, right from any issue in your inbox.

## Unsubscribe

1. Open any Extra Chill newsletter email.
2. Scroll to the bottom.
3. Select **Unsubscribe**.

That's it. You won't get future issues at that email address.

## Change Your Email Address

Your subscription belongs to the email address you signed up with. To get the newsletter somewhere else:

1. Unsubscribe from an issue sent to your old address.
2. Sign up again with your new address. See [Getting the Extra Chill Newsletter](getting-the-newsletter.md).

## Deleting Your Account

If you delete your Extra Chill account, we also take your account's email address off the newsletter.

## Artist Mailing Lists Are Separate

Signing up on an artist's link page or profile puts you on that artist's own mailing list, not the Extra Chill newsletter. Unsubscribing from one doesn't change the other. To learn more, read [Artist Preferences](https://docs.extrachill.com/artist-platform/artist-preferences/).

Venue updates and other notices from your Extra Chill account are separate too. Unsubscribing from the newsletter doesn't turn them off.
