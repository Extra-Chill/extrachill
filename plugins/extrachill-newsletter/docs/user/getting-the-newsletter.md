# Getting the Extra Chill Newsletter

Get Extra Chill stories, music news, and updates delivered to your inbox. You can also read past issues on the web before you sign up.

## What you’ll receive

Each issue shares a story or update from Extra Chill. Past issues have covered music scenes, festivals, and new things to explore on the site.

To browse past issues, visit the [newsletter site](https://newsletter.extrachill.com/). When an issue arrives by email, you can also choose **Read this newsletter on the web** to open it in your browser.

## Sign up

You can use any Extra Chill newsletter form. You’ll find one in the site menu, on the [Extra Chill homepage](https://extrachill.com/), after articles, and on the [newsletter site](https://newsletter.extrachill.com/).

1. Enter your email address.
2. Select **Subscribe**.
3. Look for a message under the form confirming your signup. If that address is already subscribed, the form tells you.

These forms sign you up for the same Extra Chill newsletter. You only need to sign up once.

## Other ways to sign up

- **When creating an Extra Chill account:** Select **Email me the Extra Chill newsletter** if you want to receive it. The box is optional and starts unchecked. The newsletter goes to the email address for your account.
- **When sending a message through the [contact form](https://extrachill.com/contact-us/):** The form says that sending it will sign you up. Submitting the form adds your email address to the newsletter.

## Not getting the newsletter?

Check your spam or promotions folder. Also check that you signed up with the same email address you use for your inbox.

If a signup form says your address is already subscribed, it is already on the list. To stop future issues, see [Unsubscribing from the Newsletter](unsubscribing.md).
