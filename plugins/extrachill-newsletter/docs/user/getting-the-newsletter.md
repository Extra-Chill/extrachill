# Getting the Extra Chill Newsletter

The Extra Chill newsletter brings independent music stories, scene reflections, and news about what's new on Extra Chill straight to your inbox.

## What You Get

Each issue is a full story or update from the Extra Chill team. Past issues have covered new features like the events calendar and free link pages for artists, festival reflections, and big news about Extra Chill.

Every issue ends with links to the rest of Extra Chill, and a **Read this newsletter on the web** link if you'd rather read it in your browser.

Want to see what you're signing up for? Every past issue is on the [newsletter site](https://newsletter.extrachill.com/).

## Sign Up

There's one Extra Chill newsletter. Every signup form adds you to the same one, so you only need to sign up once.

1. Find a signup form. There's one in the site menu on every page, on the [Extra Chill homepage](https://extrachill.com/), at the end of articles, and at the top of the [newsletter site](https://newsletter.extrachill.com/).
2. Enter your email address.
3. Select **Subscribe**.

A message under the form tells you it worked. If you were already on the list, it tells you that instead.

## Other Ways to Join

- **When you create an Extra Chill account**, check **Email me the Extra Chill newsletter**. Leave it unchecked if you don't want it.
- **When you send a message** through the [contact form](https://extrachill.com/contact-us/), you're added to the newsletter. The form says so before you send.

## Not Getting It?

Check your spam or promotions folder first. Then make sure you signed up with the email address you're checking.

To stop getting the newsletter, see [Unsubscribing from the Newsletter](unsubscribing.md).
