# Find and read music stories

Reading Extra Chill stories helps you discover new music, artists, and the people behind them. You can browse the blog without an account.

## Browse the latest stories

Open **Blog** or go to the blog archive to see published articles. Choose a story title to read the full article. The archive has more than one page, so use the page links to continue browsing.

You can also start from the Extra Chill homepage and choose **Read the blog**. Browse a topic or artist page to find related coverage where those pages are available.

## What to expect

The blog archive shows published stories. Artist pages can include related articles and links to the artist's profile, events, or other coverage. Only published stories appear for readers; a contributor's submission is not necessarily published.

You do not need an account just to browse and read. If a page does not load, try opening the blog archive again or use the **Read the blog** link from the homepage.
