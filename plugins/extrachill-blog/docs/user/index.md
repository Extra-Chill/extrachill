# Extra Chill blog help

Find music stories, join a conversation, or share a story of your own. You can read the blog without an account; submitting through Artist Dispatch requires access.

## Read and take part

- [Find and read articles](find-and-read-articles.md): browse the blog and discover related coverage.
- [Comment on an article](commenting.md): see where comments appear and what to do if a form is not available.

## Share a story

- [Share your story with Artist Dispatch](submit-a-story.md): request access, write a firsthand story, and learn what happens after you send it.

## Common questions

### Do I need an account to read?

No. The blog is free to browse without signing in.

### Can every musician send an Artist Dispatch?

No. Artist Dispatch access is limited. Sign in on the Artist Dispatch page to see whether you can request access.

### Does submitting guarantee that my story will be published?

No. An editor reviews each story and may ask for changes, hold it, or decide not to publish it.

### What if I cannot comment?

Check the article for a comment form and follow its on-screen instructions. If no form is shown, comments are not available on that article right now.
