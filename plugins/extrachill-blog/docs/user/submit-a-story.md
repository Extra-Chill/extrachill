# Share a story with Extra Chill

Artist Dispatch helps musicians tell a firsthand story about making music, performing, or building a music scene. An editor reads each submission and may work with you before it is published.

## Request Artist Dispatch access

1. Open **Artist Dispatch** and choose **Log in** if asked. If you are new, choose **Join the community** and take part before requesting access.
2. On the access request form, choose the artist or project you represent.
3. Describe the story you want to tell. The description must be at least 50 characters.
4. Add an optional sample or reference link.
5. Read the guidelines and agree to the publishing terms, then choose **Request access**.

If the page says **Almost there**, use its checklist to see what is missing. The checklist can include 50 community points, a completed account setup, a confirmed account, an account in good standing, and a connected artist profile. Once you meet the requirements shown, you can request access.

An editor reviews your connection to the artist and your story idea. The page tells you when the request is being reviewed, whether it was approved, or when you can try again. Access may not be available to every account. If the site says Artist Dispatch is not available for your account, you cannot submit a new story through it now.

## Write and send your story

Once approved, open **Write an Artist Dispatch**. Read the guidelines and accept any updated terms shown before starting. You can write at your own pace, preview the finished page, and send it to an editor when ready.

Write from firsthand experience, not as a press release or a review of your own music. Check names, dates, credits, quotes, and facts. Explain your connection to the artist or project, including relevant gifts, payments, or relationships. Send original work, and get permission before sharing private conversations or media you do not own. If you later spot a mistake, tell the editorial team so it can be fixed.

You can use AI to organize notes, transcribe a conversation, or clean up a draft, but you are responsible for every fact and quote. Do not use it to invent an experience or quote. Tell your editor if AI played a major role in your story. If an editor asks for a photo or other media, send only work you own or have permission to use, name its creator, and say if an image was made with AI. Published Artist Dispatches include a note explaining your connection to the artist or project.

The writing area is for text and does not accept uploads. An editor may ask you for a photo or other material separately. You can have up to three drafts and one story waiting for review at a time; finish or submit a draft before starting another if you reach that limit.

Choose **Save Draft** to keep working later, or **Submit for Review** when your story is ready for an editor. Your Artist Dispatch page groups your work under **Drafts**, **Awaiting Review**, and **Published**. Choose **Resume** beside a draft to keep editing or **View** beside a published story to read it.

Sending a story does not guarantee publication. An editor may ask questions, suggest changes, hold the story, or decide not to publish it. You keep the copyright. If accepted, you give Extra Chill permission to edit, publish, archive, share, and promote the story. Artist Dispatch does not promise payment.

## If something goes wrong

- **Access request is still being reviewed:** The page will say **We are taking a look** while an editor reviews your artist connection and idea.
- **The page says “Almost there”:** Use the checklist to see which access requirements you still need to meet.
- **You cannot start a story:** Check the page for an access decision or updated guidelines you need to accept. If you see **Please review the updated guidelines**, read and accept the current guidelines before starting your next story.
- **You already have too many drafts:** Finish or submit an existing draft before starting another.
- **The page says the writing tool is temporarily unavailable:** Try again later. Your approval and existing stories are safe.
- **Artist Dispatch is taking a break:** New access requests are not being accepted right now. You can still use the rest of Extra Chill as usual.
- **The page says access is not available for your account:** You cannot submit a new story right now. Anything already published stays online.
