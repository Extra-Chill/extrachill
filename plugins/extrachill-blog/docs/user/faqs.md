# Blog questions

## Do I need an account to read?

No. The blog is free to browse without signing in.

## Can every musician send an Artist Dispatch?

No. Artist Dispatch access is limited. Sign in on the Artist Dispatch page to see whether you can request access.

## Does submitting guarantee that my story will be published?

No. An editor reviews each story and may ask for changes, hold it, or decide not to publish it.

## What if I cannot comment?

Check the article for a comment form and follow its on-screen instructions. If no form is shown, comments are not available on that article right now.
