# Blog questions

## Do I need an account to read?

No. The blog is free to browse without signing in.

## Can every musician send an Artist Dispatch?

No. Artist Dispatch access is limited. Sign in on the Artist Dispatch page to see whether you can request access.

## Does submitting guarantee that my story will be published?

No. An editor reviews each story and may ask for changes, hold it, or decide not to publish it.

## What do I need to request Artist Dispatch access?

The checklist on the Artist Dispatch page shows what your account still needs. It can include 50 community points, completed account setup, a confirmed account, an account in good standing, and a connected artist profile.

## What if I cannot request access?

If the page says **Almost there**, use its checklist. If Artist Dispatch is taking a break, new requests are not being accepted right now. If it says Artist Dispatch is not available for your account, you cannot submit a new story right now; anything already published stays online.

## What if I cannot comment?

Check the article for a comment form and follow its on-screen instructions. If no form is shown, comments are not available on that article right now.
