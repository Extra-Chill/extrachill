# What the Festival Wire Is

The Festival Wire keeps you up to date on music festivals without checking a dozen sites. Lineup drops, schedule announcements, ticket news, and what fans are saying about each festival land in one place.

Find it on the [Extra Chill News Wire](https://wire.extrachill.com/), or go straight to the [full Festival Wire](https://wire.extrachill.com/festival-wire/).

## Where the News Comes From

Festival Wire stories are short news updates put together automatically from festival announcements and public fan discussions. New stories arrive throughout the day. The top of the Festival Wire shows when it was last updated.

Each story says where the news came from, and most end with a **Source** link so you can read the original announcement or discussion yourself. If a story matters to your plans, like set times or ticket details, check the source or the festival's own site before you act on it.

## Reading a Story

Each story shows the festival and location it's about. Select either one to see more news for that festival or that place.

Under the story you'll find:

- **From Around the Extra Chill Network**, with links to related articles, events, and community discussions about the same festival or place.
- **Related news** for the same festival, so you can catch up on everything leading up to it.

To browse by festival or location, see [Finding Festival News](finding-festival-news.md).
