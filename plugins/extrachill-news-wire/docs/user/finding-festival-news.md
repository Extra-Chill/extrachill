# Finding Festival News

Only care about one festival, or festivals near you? Narrow the Festival Wire down to just the news you want.

## Filter the Festival Wire

1. Open the [Festival Wire](https://wire.extrachill.com/festival-wire/).
2. Choose a festival from **Festival**. You can type in the search box above the list to find it faster.
3. Choose a place from **Location**, if you like.
4. Select **Apply Filters**.

To see everything again, select **Reset Filters**.

You can use one filter or both. Only festivals and locations that have news show up in the lists.

## Festival Pages

Every festival has its own page with all of its news in one place. Open it by selecting the festival's name on any story. For example, here's the [Bonnaroo page](https://wire.extrachill.com/festival/bonnaroo/).

When we know them, the top of a festival page shows the festival's **Dates** and **Location**. Select the location to see news from other festivals in the same place.

## Save Your Search

A filtered Festival Wire has its own web address. Bookmark it, and you can check your favorite festival's news any time.
