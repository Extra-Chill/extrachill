# Understanding Link Page Analytics

See which links fans choose and when they visit your page. Use these numbers to decide what to feature and whether a recent promotion brought more visits.

## Open your results

1. Sign in at [artist.extrachill.com](https://artist.extrachill.com).
2. Open **Analytics** from your artist dashboard.
3. Choose the artist and date range you want to review.

## Read the numbers

- **Page views** count visits to your link page. Repeat visits can count more than once, so views are not a count of different people.
- **Link clicks** count clicks on the links in your page's main content.
- **Top links** show which destinations received the most clicks.
- **Click rate** compares clicks with views. It does not tell you how many people bought a ticket or listened to a song after leaving your page.

The chart shows changes across the selected dates. A rise after you share your page can help you spot a useful time or place to promote it.

## Use what you learn

Move an important link higher if fans are missing it. Give links clear labels that tell people where they will go. Try one change at a time, then compare the results over similar date ranges.

Results cover up to the last 90 days. Save a record of important results if you need to keep them longer.

If there are no results yet, check that you chose the right artist and date range. New pages need visits before there is activity to show. Share and open your public page, then allow time for new results to appear.

Built-in results do not show what fans do after they leave your link page. A click is not proof of a purchase or a new subscriber.
