# Creating Your Artist Merch Store

Sell merchandise from your artist's shop and manage products, orders, shipping, and payments in one place.

## Set up your shop

1. Sign in and open [Shop Manager](https://artist.extrachill.com/manage-shop/).
2. Choose the artist whose shop you want to manage.
3. Open **Shipping**, enter your shipping address, and choose **Save Address**.
4. Open **Payments** and follow the prompts to connect your payment account and provide the requested business and bank details.
5. Return to the shop and refresh the payment status. Make sure the account can receive payments before publishing products.

If the account is pending or restricted, finish the requested steps before trying to sell.

## Add merchandise

Open **Products** and choose **Add Product**. Enter a name, price above zero, description, and at least one product image. You can add a sale price when needed.

For products with sizes, turn on the size option and enter stock for each size. Otherwise, enter a single stock quantity. Review your pictures, price, and stock before choosing **Published**. Use **Draft** if the product is not ready for customers.

Use the product's edit control to change details later. Choose **View Shop** to check your public storefront.

## Fulfill orders

Open **Orders** and select an order to see the items and shipping address. For an order needing fulfillment, use **Print Shipping Label** when available and review the shipping details before purchasing. You can return to the order to reprint its label.

After sending the package, check or enter its tracking number and choose **Mark as Shipped**. Do not mark an order shipped before handing it over for delivery.

If you need to refund an order, use **Refund Order** and review the amount before confirming. For questions about an order or payment, visit the [Tech Support Forum](https://community.extrachill.com/r/tech-support).
