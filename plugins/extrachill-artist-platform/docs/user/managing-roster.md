# Managing Your Artist Team

Invite band members or trusted helpers to share the work of keeping your artist profile and link page up to date.

## Invite a profile manager

1. Sign in to the [profile manager](https://artist.extrachill.com/manage-artist-profiles/).
2. Choose your artist and open **Profile Managers**.
3. Enter the person's email address. Choose their account if it appears in the suggestions.
4. Choose **Send Invite**.

Pending invitations appear in the list until accepted. Invitees receive an email with a link to join. People without an account need to create one first.

## Accept an invitation

Open the link in your invitation email and sign in or register when asked. Use the account intended for the invitation. If the link is invalid or expired, ask the person who invited you for a new invitation.

## Remove a manager

Find the person in **Profile Managers** and choose **Remove**. They will no longer be able to manage this artist's profile or link page through that membership. Removing a manager does not delete the artist's pages.

If you cannot access the artist you expected to manage, check that you are signed in to the right account. Ask an existing manager to check your membership, or visit the [Tech Support Forum](https://community.extrachill.com/r/tech-support).
