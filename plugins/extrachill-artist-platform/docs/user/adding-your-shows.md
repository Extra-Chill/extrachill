# Adding Your Shows

Get your shows on the Extra Chill Events Calendar so fans can find them, mark them Going, and see them on your artist profile. You can also let local shows know you're up for an opening slot.

## Open the Shows Tab

1. Sign in to the [profile manager](https://artist.extrachill.com/manage-artist).
2. Choose your artist.
3. Open **Shows**.

The tab gives you two ways to add shows: one at a time, or a whole calendar at once.

## Add One Show

1. In **Shows**, choose **Add a show**. The Submit Event page opens on the Events Calendar.
2. **Have a flyer?** Upload a JPG, PNG, WebP, or PDF. With a flyer, the rest of the form is optional. We read the title, date, and venue from it.
3. **No flyer?** Enter the **Event Title** and **Event Date**. Add the **Show Time** (when the music starts, not when doors open), the **Venue**, **City / Region**, and a **Ticket or Info Link** if you have them.
4. Type your artist name under **Lineup / Headliners** so the show can be matched to your artist.
5. Complete the security check and send it.

Our team reviews shows before they appear on the calendar. Once a show is on the calendar and matched to your artist, it shows up in the **Shows** section of your artist profile. For more detail, see [Submit a Show](https://docs.extrachill.com/events-calendar/submitting-an-event).

## Import Your Whole Calendar

Playing a lot of dates? Give us the page where you already list them, and new shows land on the calendar on their own.

1. In **Shows**, choose **Import a show source**. The Submit Event page opens at **Have a reliable event source?**
2. Paste the link to your tour page and choose **Try URL**.
3. Look over the shows we found. If they look right, choose **Submit for review**.

The page needs at least two upcoming shows. After it's approved, we check it regularly and add new dates as you post them. See [Add Your Tour or Venue Calendar](https://docs.extrachill.com/events-calendar/add-your-tour) for what kinds of pages work.

## If Your Profile Has No Shows

When your artist has no upcoming shows, you and your other managers see "No upcoming shows listed" with an **Add a show** button in the **Shows** section of your public profile. Fans only see the **Shows** section once your artist has shows listed.

## Set Your Hometown

Your hometown is the Local Scene your artist belongs to. It's used for local opening slots, and it's separate from where you or your bandmates live.

1. Open **Info**.
2. Under **Local Scene**, start typing your city and pick it from the search results.
3. Choose **Save**.

You have to pick a city from the results. If you type a name without picking one, you'll see "Select a Local Scene from the search results before saving."

## Get Considered for Local Opening Slots

Want to open for touring acts in your town? Let Extra Chill know you're available.

1. Open **Local Support**.
2. Check **Available for local support opportunities**.
3. Choose **Save Local Support Settings**.

The tab shows the Local Scene you'll be considered for. It's your artist's hometown from **Info**. If your artist doesn't have one yet, your own Local Scene is used and saved as the artist's hometown. If neither is set, pick a hometown in **Info** first.

Your contact information is never shared when your artist is suggested for a show. It's only shared after you agree to it for a specific request.

To stop, uncheck **Available for local support opportunities** and save. Your hometown stays as it is.
