# Managing your link page

Your link page gives people one address for your music, tickets, shop, and social pages. Keep the most useful destinations near the top so visitors can find them quickly.

## Add and arrange destinations

1. Sign in at [your artist dashboard](https://artist.extrachill.com) and open the link page editor.
2. Choose the artist you want to update.
3. Open **Links**, then choose **Add Section** if you want a heading to group destinations.
4. Choose **Add Link**. Enter a short title and the full web address.
5. Drag sections and links into the order you want people to see them.
6. Choose **Save changes** and wait for **Saved!**
7. Open your public page and test each address.

Section headings are optional. Remove a link or section if you no longer need it. Check carefully before removing; the page will no longer show it after saving.

## Add social links

Open **Socials**, choose **Add Social Link**, and select the account type. Enter the address for that account, arrange the icons, and choose whether they appear above or below your links. Save and check the public page. Use a complete, working address so visitors reach the right place.

## Choose the page style

Open **Customize** to change the title and body fonts, picture shape and size, background, colors, and button style. The preview shows how your choices fit together. Save changes, then check the public page on a phone as well as a larger screen.

The editor provides a **QR Code** button for a scannable version of your page address. Test it with a phone before printing it on posters or merchandise.

## Choose how people sign up

In **Advanced**, choose a subscribe icon, a signup form below your links, or no signup option. You can edit the form description. People who sign up this way join your artist's mailing list. Manage that list in **Subscribers** in the profile manager.

A signup does not enroll someone in an Extra Chill newsletter. Account notices and mailing list signups are separate choices.

## If your changes do not appear

Wait for **Saved!** before leaving the editor. If a message says saving failed, correct anything it calls out and try again. Check that you selected the intended artist. Reload the public page and check again; if a destination fails, correct its address and save.

For time-limited destinations and other choices, see [Advanced link page features](link-page-advanced-features.md). To understand visits and clicks, see [Link page analytics](understanding-link-page-analytics.md).
