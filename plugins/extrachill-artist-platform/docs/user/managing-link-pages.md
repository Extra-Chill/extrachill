# Managing Your Link Page

Your extrachill.link page gives fans one place to find your music, tickets, merchandise, and social pages.

## Add and arrange links

1. Sign in at [artist.extrachill.com](https://artist.extrachill.com) and open your link page editor.
2. Choose the artist you want to edit.
3. Open **Links** and choose **Add Link**.
4. Enter a clear title and the full destination web address.
5. Arrange links and sections in the order you want fans to see them.
6. Choose **Save changes**.

Keep your most important link near the top. Open your public page and test each destination after saving.

## Change the look

Use **Customize** to choose colors, fonts, picture shape, and background. Use **Socials** to add links to your social profiles. Check the preview as you make changes, then save.

## Invite fans to subscribe

In **Advanced**, choose whether to show a subscribe icon, a signup form below your links, or neither. You can also change the signup description.

Fans who submit this form join your artist's mailing list, not an Extra Chill newsletter. View and export the list from **Subscribers** in your artist profile manager.

For limited-time links and other options, see [Advanced Link Page Features](link-page-advanced-features.md). To see which links fans choose, see [Link Page Analytics](understanding-link-page-analytics.md).

## If something looks wrong

Wait for the saved message before leaving the editor. If saving fails, read the message and retry. Reopen your public page to check the result. If a link does not open, check its destination address and save the correction.
