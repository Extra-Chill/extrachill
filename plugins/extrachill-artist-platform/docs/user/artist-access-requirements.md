# Artist Access

Artist access lets musicians and people who represent them create artist profiles and link pages. You can manage separate profiles for different projects.

## Join as an artist

Start at [Join Extra Chill](https://extrachill.link/join) and follow the prompts. This joining process gives you artist access and helps you create your first profile and link page. Sign in first if you already have an Extra Chill account.

## Request access from an existing account

1. Sign in to [Extra Chill Community](https://community.extrachill.com).
2. Open your account settings and choose **Artist Platform**.
3. Choose whether you are a musician or work in the music industry.
4. Choose **Request Access**.

The Extra Chill team reviews these requests. An approval email explains how to get started. Do not create extra accounts while waiting. If you need help with a request, use the [Tech Support Forum](https://community.extrachill.com/r/tech-support).

## What access includes

You can update your artist's name, bio, pictures, and location; build a link page; view page visits and link clicks; invite profile managers; and collect and export direct artist email signups.

Being able to create artists does not give you control over someone else's profile. Ask an existing profile manager to invite you if you need to help manage their artist.

You do not need artist access to browse artists or take part in the community.
