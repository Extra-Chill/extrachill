# Managing Your Artist Profile

Keep your artist's bio, pictures, and contact with fans up to date from the [profile manager](https://artist.extrachill.com/manage-artist-profiles/).

## Update your details

1. Sign in and choose the artist you want to edit.
2. Open **Info**.
3. Update your artist name, city or region, genre, and bio.
4. Choose a profile picture and header image. Use **Remove** to clear a picture you no longer want.
5. Choose **Save** and wait for the saved message.
6. Choose **View Profile** to check the public result.

If saving fails, check the message and try again before leaving the page. Your artist name cannot be empty.

## Share the work

Open **Profile Managers** to invite or remove people who help manage your artist. See [Managing Your Team](managing-roster.md) for the steps. Only invite people you trust to edit your artist's pages.

## View and export subscribers

Open **Subscribers** to see people who signed up directly through your artist profile or link page. This artist-owned list is separate from Extra Chill newsletters and account notifications.

Use **Prev** and **Next** to browse the list. Choose **Export CSV** to download new subscribers you have not exported before. The download is a spreadsheet you can use with your mailing service. The export button is unavailable when there are no subscribers to export.

Switching artists changes the details and subscriber list you are viewing. Check the selected artist before making changes or downloading a list.
