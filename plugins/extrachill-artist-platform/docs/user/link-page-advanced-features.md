# Advanced Link Page Features

Use your link page's **Advanced** tab to keep promotions timely, send fans straight to one destination, and measure your own ads. Choose **Save changes** and wait for **Saved!** after editing these options.

## Set an end date for a link

1. Turn on **Enable Link Expiration Dates** in **Advanced**.
2. Open **Links**. Each link now has an expiration date and time.
3. Set the date and time for the link you want to end, then save.

Use this for ticket sales or offers that shouldn't stay on your page. Once the date passes, the link is removed from your page for good. Keep a copy of any address you may want to add again later.

## Send visitors straight to one address

1. Turn on **Enable Temporary Redirect**.
2. Enter the full web address under **Redirect URL**.
3. Save your changes.

Visitors to your link page address go straight to that address instead of seeing your full page. To show your full page again, turn the redirect off and save. Test your public address after either change.

## Choose how videos open

Video links can play right on your link page. To open them away from your page instead, turn on the option to disable the inline video player and save.

## Add your own ad and analytics tracking

Running ads for a release or a tour? Add your own tracking IDs so your ads account and your analytics account can count visits to your link page. Find each ID in the account you use for ads or analytics.

The last two boxes in **Advanced** hold your tracking IDs:

1. **Ad tracking ID:** in the first of the two boxes, paste the ID from your ads account. It's a number made of digits only.
2. **Analytics tag ID:** in the second box, paste the tag ID from your analytics account. It starts with **G-** or **AW-**, such as G-AB12CD34.
3. Choose **Save changes**.

Each time someone opens your public link page, it's counted in those accounts. If an ID isn't in the right format, nothing is added to your page, so double-check it if you don't see visits. Clear a box and save to stop tracking with that account.

You don't need your own tracking to see visits and clicks. Extra Chill already counts them for you. See [Understanding Link Page Analytics](https://docs.extrachill.com/artist-platform/understanding-link-page-analytics).

## Choose your email signup display

Email signups have their own **Newsletter** tab. Under **How fans sign up**, choose **Sign-up form on the page**, **Bell icon (opens a sign-up form)**, or **Off**. Use **Message to fans** to tell people what you'll send them.

Signups belong to your artist's mailing list. They don't subscribe fans to Extra Chill newsletters.

## Make your page easy to share

Use **Customize** for fonts, colors, buttons, and backgrounds. Use **Socials** for your social profile links. The editor's **QR Code** button lets you download a scannable square for posters or merchandise. Test a printed copy with your phone before printing a large batch.

The public page's share button lets visitors copy or share your address.
