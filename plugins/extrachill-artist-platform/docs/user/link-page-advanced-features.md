# Advanced Link Page Features

Use your link page's **Advanced** tab to keep promotions timely, send fans straight to a destination, and choose how they join your mailing list. Save your changes after editing these options.

## Set an end date for a link

1. Turn on **Enable Link Expiration Dates** in **Advanced**.
2. Open **Links** and edit the link you want to end.
3. Set its expiration date and save.

Use this for ticket sales or offers that should not stay on your page. Keep a copy of any destination you may want to add again later.

## Send visitors straight to one link

Turn on **Enable Temporary Redirect** and choose one of your existing links as the destination. Save your changes. Visitors to your link page address will go straight to that destination instead of seeing your full page.

To show your full page again, turn the redirect off and save. Test your public address after either change.

## Choose how videos open

YouTube links can play directly on your link page. To open them away from your page instead, turn on **Disable Inline YouTube Video Player** and save.

## Choose your email signup display

Under **Subscription Settings**, choose the subscribe icon, the form below your links, or the option to hide signup. You can also edit the form description to tell fans what you will send them.

Signups belong to your artist's mailing list. They do not subscribe fans to Extra Chill newsletters, and following an artist does not add a fan's account email to this list.

## Make your page easy to share

Use **Customize** for fonts, colors, buttons, and backgrounds. Use **Socials** for your social profile links. The editor's **QR Code** button lets you download a scannable square for posters or merchandise. Test a printed copy with your phone before printing a large batch.

The public page's share button lets visitors copy or share your address. You do not need any extra tracking setup to view the built-in [page analytics](understanding-link-page-analytics.md).
