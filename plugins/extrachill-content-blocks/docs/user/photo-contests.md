# Enter and Vote in Photo Contests

Extra Chill photo contests let you show off your best shot, like a Halloween costume at a show, and get your friends to vote for it. Anyone can enter, anyone can vote, and every entry gets its own link and QR code so you can rally votes from the crowd or from your phone.

## Enter a contest

1. Open the contest page and select the upload button, such as **Upload your costume photo**.
2. Choose a photo. You can use a JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, or HEIC photo up to 10 MB. Big phone photos are resized automatically so they upload faster.
3. Enter a **Name**. This is the name voters see on your entry.
4. Enter your **Email**. This is how Extra Chill reaches you if you win. It is never shown on your entry.
5. Add your **Instagram** handle if you want. It appears on your entry as a link to your profile.
6. Complete the quick check that you're a person, if one appears.
7. Select the button to enter, such as **Enter my costume**.

Keep the screen open while your photo uploads. On cell data at a crowded venue, this can take up to 30 seconds.

Your entry goes up on the contest page right away. You see a "You're in!" message with your photo, your share link, and your QR code. Some contests also email you a link to your entry.

## Contest rules to know

- **One entry per email per contest.** If you try to enter again with the same email, you see your existing entry instead.
- **The same photo can't be entered twice** in one contest. Try a different shot.
- **Your name, photo, and Instagram handle are public.** Your email is not.

## Get votes for your entry

Every entry has its own link and QR code. After you enter, your entry shows at the top of the contest page whenever you come back on the same device, along with your vote count.

- **Share my link** opens your phone's share menu so you can send your entry to friends or post it. On a computer, the button says **Copy my link** instead.
- **The QR code** sits in the corner of your photo. Tap it to make it bigger, then hold up your screen so people nearby can scan it and vote.

Anyone who opens your link or scans your code lands on your entry, with a big **Vote** button. They can select **See all entries** to browse the rest of the contest.

Lost your link on a new phone? Enter the contest again with the same email. Instead of a second entry, you get your original entry and its link back.

## Vote for your favorites

1. Find an entry you like and select **Vote**.
2. Enter your email the first time you vote.
3. Select **Vote** to confirm.

Your vote counts right away and the button changes to **Voted ✓**.

- **One vote per email per entry.** You can vote for as many different entries as you like.
- **After your first vote, voting is one tap.** Your email is remembered on that device, so other **Vote** buttons count your vote right away. If you're signed in to your Extra Chill account, your account email is used.
- **Some contests send a quick thank-you email** after your first vote.

Entries appear in a fresh, shuffled order each time the page loads, so everyone gets a fair look. A **Leading right now** row near the top shows the entries with the most votes so far.

## When contests open and close

Each contest can have its own times for entering and voting.

- **Before a contest opens,** you see "Contest opens" with the date and time. The entry form and **Vote** buttons appear once each part opens.
- **When entries close,** you see a message telling you whether voting is still open or has ended.
- **When voting closes,** the **Vote** buttons change to **Voting Closed**.
- **Organizers can close entries or voting early.**
- **Some contests show finalists** after voting closes. The top entries by vote count appear in ranked order under **Finalists**.

The contest page also shows details for the related event, with links to **Get Tickets** or **Event Details** when they're available.

## Keeping contests fair and friendly

Contest moderators can hide entries that break the rules or don't belong. A hidden entry disappears from the contest page and can't receive votes.

## What happens with your email

When you enter or vote, Extra Chill uses your email in a few ways:

- **Photo contest emails.** Your email is added to Extra Chill's photo contest mailing list.
- **Your Extra Chill account.** Your email is linked to an Extra Chill account. If you already have one, it's used. If not, a new account is set up for you that you can claim later. You may get an email with a link to set a password and claim it. To learn more about accounts, see [Create an account or sign in](https://docs.extrachill.com/accounts/account-access).

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time. See [Unsubscribing from the Newsletter](https://docs.extrachill.com/newsletter/unsubscribing).

## If something goes wrong

- **The upload failed.** Check your connection and try again. Make sure your photo is one of the supported types and under 10 MB.
- **"Could not load entries."** Refresh the page.
- **The QR code didn't load.** Select **Try again**, or share your link instead.
- **Your entry disappeared.** A moderator may have hidden it. Contact Extra Chill if you think this was a mistake.
- **You want your entry taken down.** Contact Extra Chill and ask for it to be removed.
