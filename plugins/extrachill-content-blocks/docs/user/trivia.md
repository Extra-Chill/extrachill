# Play Music Trivia

Think you know your favorite band inside and out? Some Extra Chill articles include interactive music trivia, like [Grateful Dead trivia for true Deadheads](https://extrachill.com/grateful-dead-trivia). Answer each question right on the page and see your score add up as you go.

You don't need an account or an email address to play.

## How to play

1. Read the question.
2. Select the answer you think is right.
3. See how you did. Your answer is marked right or wrong, and the correct answer is highlighted if you missed it.
4. Read the explanation, if there is one. Many questions share a fun fact or the story behind the answer.
5. Keep going through the rest of the questions in the article.

Your first pick is your final answer. Once you choose, that question locks.

## Your score

A running score above the first question shows how many you've gotten right so far, such as 3/15.

When you've answered every question, your final result appears below the last one. It shows your score, the percent you got right, and a title based on how you did. Each quiz has its own titles, so a top score might call you a "Trivia Master" or something that fits the topic.

## Play again

Your answers aren't saved. Refresh the page to clear your answers and start the quiz over, or send the article to a friend and compare scores.
