# Band Name Generator and Rapper Name Generator

Stuck on a name for your band or your rap alter ego? Extra Chill's free name generators mix your own name or word with hand-picked words to make thousands of possible names. Some are silly, some might end up on a marquee. Keep pressing the button until one sticks.

You don't need an account or an email address to use either generator.

## Band Name Generator

Use the [Extra Chill Band Name Generator](https://extrachill.com/band-name-generator/) to come up with band name ideas in the style of your favorite genre.

1. Type your name or any word in **Your Name/Word**. It always goes at the start of your band name.
2. Pick a **Genre**: Rock, Country, Metal, Indie, Punk, Jam, Electronic, or Random. Random mixes words from every genre, plus a few wild cards.
3. Pick the **Number of Words** to add after your word: 2, 3, or 4.
4. Check **Add "The" at the beginning** if you want "The" in front of the generated words.
5. Check **Add "& The" in the middle** if you want a name like "Something & The Something."
6. Select **Generate Band Name**.

Your band name appears below the form. Press the button again for a new one, or change your choices and try again.

## Rapper Name Generator

Use the [Extra Chill Rapper Name Generator](https://extrachill.com/rap-name-generator/) to find a stage name that fits your style.

1. Type your real name, nickname, or any word in **Your Name**. It's worked into your rapper name.
2. Pick a **Gender**: Non-binary, Male, or Female. This changes the titles you might get, like "King," "Queen," or "MC."
3. Pick a **Style**: Random, Old School, Trap, Grime, or Conscious. Each style uses its own words and can put your name in a different spot.
4. Pick the **Number of Words**: 2 or 3. Three words makes a longer name, sometimes with "the" in the middle.
5. Select **Generate Rapper Name**.

Your rapper name appears below the form. Don't love it? Press the button again or try a different style.

## Tips

- **Try a few genres or styles** with the same word. The results change a lot.
- **Use a nickname or a favorite word** instead of your real name for something more unexpected.
- **Every press makes a new name,** so write down the ones you like before you move on.
- **Check before you commit.** Do a quick search to make sure no one else is already using your favorite name.
