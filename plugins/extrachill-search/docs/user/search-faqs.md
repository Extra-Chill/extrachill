# Search FAQs

Quick answers to common questions about finding things on Extra Chill.

## Why am I only seeing results from one site?

Search looks through the site you're on unless you pick otherwise. Select the magnifying glass at the top of the page, type your search, choose **Entire network**, and search again. Each result has a label showing which site it came from.

## Which sites can I search?

You can search Extra Chill, Extra Chill Community, Extra Chill Events, Extra Chill Artist Platform, Extra Chill Shop, Extra Chill News Wire, Extra Chill Newsletter, and Extra Chill Documentation. Pick **This site** to search just the one you're on, or **Entire network** to search them all together.

## Can I search the forums?

Yes. Search from the community site with **This site** picked, or pick **Entire network** from anywhere. Forum replies appear as **Re:** followed by the topic's title, and opening one takes you right to that reply.

## Can I find shows and concerts?

Yes. Shows on the events calendar appear in search with the **Extra Chill Events** label. Try a venue name, a city, or an artist. Older shows can appear too, so check the date on each result. To browse what's coming up, see the [events calendar help](https://docs.extrachill.com/events-calendar).

## Can I find an artist's link page?

Link pages don't appear in search. Search for the artist's name to find their artist profile instead.

## Why did my search find nothing?

A few things to try:

- Check the spelling.
- Use whole words. Partial words like "guita" won't match "guitar."
- Use fewer, more specific words.
- Search again with **Entire network** picked.

## Why do some results only match part of my search?

When nothing contains every word you typed, search shows results that match some of your words instead. Add or swap a word to narrow things down.

## How are results ordered?

The closest matches come first. An exact title match ranks highest, followed by titles containing your words, then results that mention your words often. Newer content gets a small boost when matches are close.

## Do I need an account to search?

No. Anyone can search Extra Chill without signing in.

## Can I see more than one page of results?

Yes. Use the page numbers at the bottom of the results. Your choice of one site or every site stays the same as you move between pages.

## Why can't I find a post I just wrote?

Only published content appears in search. If something is still a draft, it won't show up until it's published.
