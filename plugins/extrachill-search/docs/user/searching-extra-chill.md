# Search Extra Chill

Search helps you find articles, forum conversations, upcoming shows, artists, merch, newsletter issues, festival news, and help articles. One search box can look through the site you're on or through every Extra Chill site at once.

## Start a search

1. Select the magnifying glass icon at the top of any Extra Chill page. A search panel opens.
2. Type what you're looking for, like a band name, a city, a venue, or a topic.
3. Choose where to search:
   - **This site** looks only through the site you're on. The site's name appears next to the option, so you always know which one it is.
   - **Entire network** looks through every Extra Chill site at once.
4. Press Enter or select the search button.

**This site** is picked for you unless you choose otherwise. If you're on the forums, you'll see forum results first. If you want everything, pick **Entire network** before you search.

To close the panel without searching, select the **X** in the corner.

You'll also find the same search box, with the same two choices, on any page that can't be found and on a results page with no matches.

## What each site adds to your results

When you search every Extra Chill site, here's what you can find:

- **Extra Chill:** music articles, interviews, and reviews
- **Extra Chill Community:** forums, forum topics, and replies
- **Extra Chill Events:** shows and concerts on the calendar
- **Extra Chill Artist Platform:** artist profiles and artist forum posts
- **Extra Chill Shop:** products
- **Extra Chill News Wire:** festival news
- **Extra Chill Newsletter:** past newsletter issues
- **Extra Chill Documentation:** help articles like this one

Only published content shows up. Drafts and other unpublished work never appear in search. Artist link pages don't appear in search, but the artist's profile does.

## Read your results

The results page says **Search Results for:** followed by what you typed. Each result shows:

- A label with the name of the site it came from, like **Extra Chill Events** or **Extra Chill Community**
- Its image, if it has one
- The title, date, and author
- A **View Full Post** button that opens it

Forum replies show up as **Re:** followed by the topic's title. Opening one takes you straight to that reply inside the conversation.

If there are more results than fit on one page, use the page numbers at the bottom to see more. Your choice of this site or every site stays the same as you move between pages.

## How results are ordered

The best matches come first:

1. A title that matches your search exactly
2. Titles that contain your exact words in order, with a boost when the title starts with them
3. Titles that contain all of your words
4. Results that mention your words more often

Newer content gets a small boost, so a fresh article can rank above an older one that matches just as well.

## Get better results

- **Type whole words.** A search for "guita" won't find "guitar."
- **Use a few specific words.** Every word you type has to appear for a result to count as a full match. A band name plus a city usually works better than a long sentence.
- **Check your spelling.** If nothing matches, you'll see a message asking you to check spelling or try different words, along with a fresh search box.
- **Try every site.** If your search comes up empty on the site you're on, search again and pick **Entire network**.
- **Don't worry about symbols.** Punctuation like parentheses and plus signs is ignored. Curly and straight apostrophes count as the same thing.

If no result contains all of your words, search shows results that match some of them instead. Those results may be less on target, so try adding or swapping a word.
