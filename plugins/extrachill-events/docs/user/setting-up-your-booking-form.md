# Setting Up Your Booking Form

Get the details you need from artists the first time they ask, so you spend less time chasing answers. You choose the questions, the rooms artists can ask for, and how long you hold a date.

Booking inquiries are open to a small number of venues right now. To take inquiries for your room, [contact Extra Chill](https://extrachill.com/contact-us/).

To get started:

1. Log in and open [Venue settings](https://events.extrachill.com/venue-settings/).
2. Pick your venue.
3. Open the **Booking Form** tab or the **Booking Rules** tab.

## Booking Form Tab

### Questions

Every form already asks for:

- The requested date
- The artist or project name
- A contact name and email
- "What is your vision for the show?"

To ask more:

1. Select **Add a question**.
2. Type the question.
3. Pick an answer type: **Short answer**, **Long answer**, **Link**, or **Choose one**.
4. For **Choose one**, list the choices under **Answers to choose from**, one per line.
5. Check **Required** if artists must answer it.
6. Select **Save booking form**.

To drop a question, select **Remove**. If another question depends on its answer, you'll need to change that one first.

The **Preview** shows your form the way artists will see it. If anything needs fixing before you can save, it's listed at the bottom.

### Put the Form on Your Own Website

Artists can find your form on your Extra Chill venue page. You can also add it to your own website.

1. Under **Put this booking form on your website**, enter **Your venue website address**. Add one website per line. The form will only work on the websites you list.
2. Select **Save booking form**.
3. Select **Copy website code**.
4. Paste it into a page on your website, or send it to whoever runs your website.

Entering your address doesn't add the form for you. It only shows up once the copied code is on your website.

## Booking Rules Tab

### Turn Inquiries On or Off

Under **Booking operation**, check **Accept booking inquiries** to take inquiries. Uncheck it to stop. When it's off, the **Submit a booking inquiry** button disappears from your venue page.

### Spaces

If you have more than one room or stage, add each one as a space. Artists pick a space when they ask for a date, and each space keeps its own calendar. Two artists can be held on the same night if they're in different rooms.

1. Select **Add space**.
2. Give it a **Name**, like "Main Room" or "Patio Stage".
3. Pick one **Default space**. The form picks it for artists first, and they can change it.

You need at least one space before artists can check whether a date is open.

### How Long a Hold Lasts

Pick a **Default hold duration**: 1 hour, 4 hours, 12 hours, 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, or 14 days. When you hold a date for an artist, it stays held this long. Then it opens up again.

### Default Deal

Set the terms you usually offer, so you don't start from scratch every time:

- **Deal type:** **Custom terms**, **Guarantee**, or **Door split**.
- **Guarantee:** the set amount you pay.
- **Revenue share (%)** and **Revenue basis:** your split, and what it's based on.
- **Currency.**

These are only starting terms. Each booking keeps its own deal, so you can change the terms for one show without changing the rest.

### Private Booking Files

You'll see options for collecting files like press kits, stage plots, and riders. File sharing isn't open yet, so saving these choices won't turn it on.

### Save Your Rules

Select **Save settings** when you're done.
