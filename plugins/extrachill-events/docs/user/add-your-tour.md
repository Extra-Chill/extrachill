# Add Your Tour or Venue Calendar

Play a lot of shows? Don't send them one at a time. Give Extra Chill the page where you already list your dates, and new shows land on the calendar automatically.

## What Works

A page that lists upcoming dates for one artist or one venue:

- An artist's tour page
- A venue's calendar on the venue's own website

The page needs at least two upcoming shows. A page for a single event isn't enough on its own. Use the [event form](https://events.extrachill.com/submit/) for one show.

## Add Your Page

1. Log in to Extra Chill, or join for free.
2. Open [Submit Event](https://events.extrachill.com/submit/).
3. Paste the page link under **Have a reliable event source?** and select **Try URL**.
4. Look over the shows we found. You'll see a count and a preview of the first few dates.
5. If they look right, select **Submit for review**.

You'll get an email confirming we received the page.

## Pages We Can't Use

The form checks your page right away and tells you if it can't be added. That happens when:

- We found fewer than two upcoming shows on it.
- It mixes many artists across different venues, so the shows don't belong to one act or one room. Most festival schedules fall into this group.
- It lists shows at a venue but isn't that venue's own website.
- It's a ticketing site or an event listing site rather than the artist's or venue's own page.
- We already add shows from that page.

When that happens, the regular event form opens below so you can still send shows one at a time.

## What Happens Next

The team reviews the page and you get a notification on Extra Chill with the result.

- **Artist pages:** the notification says how many shows were added and links to the artist's page on the calendar.
- **Venue pages:** the notification links to the venue's page on the calendar.
- **Not approved:** the notification may include the reason, and links back to Submit Event.

After approval, we check your page regularly and add new dates as you post them. Venue calendars are usually checked every day, and artist tour pages about once a week. Keep your own page up to date and the calendar keeps up. You don't have to send us anything else.

## Shows Listed in More Than One Place

The same show often turns up on a tour page and a venue calendar. We match shows with the same title, venue, and date so each one appears once. If you still see a duplicate, post the two event links in [The Lab](https://community.extrachill.com/r/the-lab), the community help forum.

## Just One Show?

Use the event form on [Submit Event](https://events.extrachill.com/submit/). If you have a flyer, upload it and we'll read the title, date, and venue for you. See [How to Submit an Event](submitting-an-event.md).
