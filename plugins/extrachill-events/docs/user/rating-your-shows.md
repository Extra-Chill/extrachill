# Rating Your Shows

Give every concert you went to a private star rating, from 1 to 5. Over time, your ratings turn My Shows into a personal record of the nights that stuck with you and the ones that didn't.

## Rate a Show

1. Open [My Shows](https://events.extrachill.com/my-shows).
2. Choose **Past**.
3. Select **Rate** next to a show.
4. Choose from 1 to 5 stars.

Your rating saves right away. You can rate any past show in your history, including shows you added from years ago.

Only shows you have marked **I Was There** can be rated. If a concert is missing, add it first. See [Marking Shows](https://docs.extrachill.com/events-calendar/marking-shows).

## Change or Clear a Rating

- To change a rating, choose a different number of stars.
- To clear a rating, select the star you already chose. The show goes back to showing **Rate**.

## Who Can See Your Ratings

Only you. Ratings never appear on your public concert history, on event pages, or on artist pages, even when your concert history is public. Other people see the show, but not your stars.

Read [Concert History Privacy](https://docs.extrachill.com/events-calendar/concert-history-privacy) to control who can see your shows.

## Removing a Show

If you remove a show from My Shows, its rating is removed too. Adding the show back starts it without a rating.

## Upcoming Shows

Upcoming shows can't be rated yet. Once the show date passes, the show moves to **Past** and **Rate** appears.
