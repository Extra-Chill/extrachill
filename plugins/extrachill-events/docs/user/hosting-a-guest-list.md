# Hosting a Guest List

A guest list lets you put friends, press, and the band's people on the list for your show. Every guest gets their own pass that scans at the door, so there's no paper list and no guessing at names.

## Who Can Run a Guest List

Anyone on the venue's team or the promoter's team for a show can run its guest list. Management companies and booking agencies set up as a promoter on Extra Chill count as promoters.

Log in, open the show's page on the [Events Calendar](https://events.extrachill.com/), and scroll past the event details to **Manage guest list**. If you don't see it, your account isn't on the team for that show's venue or promoter.

Bands and managers who aren't on the team can still put names on the list. You can invite them, or they can ask from the guest list page. See [Inviting Bands to the Guest List](inviting-bands-to-the-guest-list.md).

## Turn It On

1. Open the show's page and find **Manage guest list**.
2. Type a **Guest list password**.
3. Set **Guest list spots**. Enter 0 for unlimited spots.
4. Choose whether bands get a set number of spots each (see Band Spots below).
5. Pick the pass image, or leave it as is.
6. Tap **Save and enable guest list**.

Once it's on, the panel shows how many passes are claimed, how many people are checked in, and how many spots are left. You also get:

- A **Share link** to the show's guest list page, such as https://events.extrachill.com/events/your-show/guest-list/. Tap **Copy share link** to paste it anywhere.
- A QR code for the same page. Tap **Download share QR** to put it on a flyer or a post.

Anyone with the share link and the password can claim a spot. You never have to share the password, though. If you'd rather control every name, keep it to yourself and add guests by name instead.

To change the password later, type a new one and save. Leave the box blank to keep the current one. Passes you already handed out keep working.

## Spots

Every name on the show's list uses one spot. That includes people who claim a spot with the password, names you add, and names bands add.

When every spot is taken, the guest list page says "The guest list is full." You can raise the number of spots at any time and save.

Your [venue or promoter list](venue-and-promoter-guest-lists.md) never uses the show's spots.

## Pass Art

Every pass shows artwork above the guest's QR code. The emailed copy of the pass shows the same image.

- **The event poster** is used by default.
- **Your own image:** under **Pass image**, choose a JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP file up to 10 MB, then save. Use **Replace pass image** to swap it later.
- **Back to the poster:** check **Remove custom image (use the event poster)** and save.
- **No image and no poster:** passes show a card with the show's title and time, like the one on the calendar.

## Add Guests by Name

Comps don't need the password. Add a name and that person is on the list.

1. Under **Add a guest**, type the **Guest name**.
2. Add their email if you have it. They'll get their pass by email.
3. Tap **Add to guest list**.

You'll see "(name) is on the list" with their pass link. Tap **Copy pass link** and text it to them so they have their pass at the door. On the door list, your comps show "Added by" with your name.

**Plus-ones:** add a second name, such as "Jake +1". Each name is its own pass and uses one spot. On the door list, "Jake +1" sits right under "Jake".

To take someone off, tap **Remove** next to their name on the door list. You can only do this before they check in. Their pass stops working right away.

## Band Spots

The box **Give each invited band a set number of spots (allotments)** decides how invited bands share the list.

- **Off:** bands can add names until the whole list is full.
- **On:** you give each band a number of spots. Bands can ask you for more.

See [Inviting Bands to the Guest List](inviting-bands-to-the-guest-list.md) for invites, requests, and approvals.

## On Show Day

On the day of the show, **Manage guest list** moves to the top of the show's page for your team. Tap **Open door list** to jump straight to the names. See [Working the Door](working-the-door.md).

## After the Show

Once the show is over, the guest list page says "This event has already happened." No one can claim new passes or send requests.
