# Getting on a Guest List

On the list for a show? Your pass lives on your phone. Show it at the door and you're in. You don't need an Extra Chill account.

## Claim a Spot with the Password

If the venue, promoter, or band gave you a guest list link and a password:

1. Open the guest list link, such as https://events.extrachill.com/events/the-show/guest-list/.
2. Enter **Your name** and the **Guest list password**.
3. Add your email if you want a copy of the pass. This is optional.
4. Tap **Get my pass**.

The page tells you how many spots are left. If you see "The guest list is full," every spot is taken. If you see "That password isn't right," check the password with whoever gave it to you.

## If the Host Manages the List

Some guest lists are run by the host only. The guest list page says "This guest list is managed by the host." There's no password to enter. Ask the venue, promoter, or band to add your name, and they'll send you a pass link.

## If Someone Added You

When a host or a band puts you on the list, you don't need the password. They'll text you a pass link or the pass will arrive by email. Open the link on your phone and your pass appears.

## Your Pass

Your pass shows your name, the show's artwork, and "You're on the list. Show this at the door:" with a QR code and a short pass code underneath.

- **It stays on your phone.** Come back to the same page on the same phone and your pass is still there. Coming back doesn't use another spot.
- **Take a screenshot** to be safe, in case you have no signal at the door.
- **It's yours alone.** Every pass has its own QR code and checks in one person, one time. Bringing someone? They need their own pass.

## Your Emailed Pass

If you added your email, or a host added it for you, you'll get an email called "Your guest list pass for" the show. It has the show's artwork, your pass code, your QR code, and an **Open my pass** button. Tap the button on your phone to keep the pass on that phone.

Lost your pass, or changed phones? Open the email or text link again.

## If Your Pass Disappears

If your pass vanishes from the page and the claim form comes back, you were taken off the list. Contact whoever put you on it.

## Venue and Promoter List Passes

Some venues and promoters keep a standing list of people who get into every show. If you're on one, you'll get an email called "Your guest list pass for" the venue or promoter. It says the pass "works at every" show of theirs "until the list changes."

Tap **Open my pass** to see your pass page. It shows your name, which list you're on, "Show this at the door at any" of their shows, your QR code, and your pass code. Bookmark it or take a screenshot. You'll use the same pass every time.

If the page says "This pass is no longer valid," you've been taken off the list.

## At the Door

Open your pass and let the door staff scan the QR code. Once you're checked in, the same pass won't work again for that show.
