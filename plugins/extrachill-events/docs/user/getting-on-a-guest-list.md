# Getting on a Guest List

On the list for a show? Your pass lives on your phone. Show it at the door and you're in. Some shows even come with a perk, like "Your first beer is on Extra Chill."

Running the list yourself? See [Hosting a Guest List](https://docs.extrachill.com/events-calendar/hosting-a-guest-list).

## Get a Pass by Tapping Going

Some shows give a pass to everyone who says they're coming. On those shows, you'll see the perk right under the **Going** button, such as "Mark yourself Going to get your pass. Your first beer is on Extra Chill."

**If you're signed in:**

1. Open the show on the [Events Calendar](https://events.extrachill.com).
2. Tap **Going**.
3. Your pass appears right under the button. We also email you a copy called "Your RSVP pass for" the show.

**If you're not signed in:**

1. Open the show and find "Enter your email to get your pass."
2. Type your **Email** and tap **Get my pass**.
3. Check your inbox. Your pass is on its way.

If you're new to Extra Chill, we make you a free account with that email so your spot is saved. The pass email has a **Set my password** link. Set a password to see your pass on the show's page and to cancel later. Lost the email? Wait ten minutes, then enter the same address again to get another copy.

## Spots

If the show has a set number of passes, you'll see how many are left, such as "12 of 50 spots left." Once every pass is taken, the show says "The guest list is full." You can still tap Going, but you won't get a pass.

## Cancel Your Pass

Can't make it? Open the show while signed in and tap **Going** again to take your mark off. Your pass stops working right away and your spot opens up for someone else.

Change your mind again? Tap **Going** while there are spots left and you'll get a pass back.

## Claim a Spot with the Password

If the venue, promoter, or band gave you a guest list link and a password:

1. Open the guest list link. It's the show's page address with "guest-list" on the end.
2. Enter **Your name** and the **Guest list password**.
3. Add your email if you want a copy of the pass. This is optional.
4. Tap **Get my pass**.

The page tells you how many spots are left. If you see "The guest list is full," every spot is taken. If you see "That password isn't right," check the password with whoever gave it to you.

## If the Host Manages the List

Some guest lists are run by the host only. The guest list page says "This guest list is managed by the host." There's no password to enter. Ask the venue, promoter, or band to add your name, and they'll send you a pass link.

## If Someone Added You

When a host or a band puts you on the list, you don't need the password. They'll text you a pass link or the pass will arrive by email. Open the link on your phone and your pass appears.

## Your Pass

A pass you got by tapping **Going** shows the perk, the show's artwork, and "Show this pass at check-in:" with a short pass code and a QR code. Open the show while signed in to see it again. Until the show is over, it also appears on that show in [My Shows](https://events.extrachill.com/my-shows) with the perk and your pass code.

A pass from a guest list link shows your name, the show's artwork, and "You're on the list. Show this at the door:" with a QR code and a short pass code underneath.

- **It stays on your phone.** Come back to the same page on the same phone and your pass is still there. Coming back doesn't use another spot.
- **Take a screenshot** to be safe, in case you have no signal at the door.
- **It's yours alone.** Every pass has its own QR code and checks in one person, one time. Bringing someone? They need their own pass.

## Your Emailed Pass

When you tap **Going**, your email is called "Your RSVP pass for" the show. It has the perk, the show's artwork, your pass code, and your QR code. Show the QR code from the email at the door, or tap **View Event** and sign in to see your pass on the show's page.

When you claim a spot from a guest list link and add your email, or a host adds it for you, you get an email called "Your guest list pass for" the show. It has the show's artwork, your pass code, your QR code, and an **Open my pass** button. Tap the button on your phone to keep the pass on that phone.

Lost your pass, or changed phones? Open the email or text link again.

## If Your Pass Disappears

If your pass vanishes from the page and the claim form comes back, you were taken off the list. Contact whoever put you on it.

## Venue and Promoter List Passes

Some venues and promoters keep a standing list of people who get into every show. If you're on one, you'll get an email called "Your guest list pass for" the venue or promoter. It says the pass "works at every" show of theirs "until the list changes."

Tap **Open my pass** to see your pass page. It shows your name, which list you're on, "Show this at the door at any" of their shows, your QR code, and your pass code. Bookmark it or take a screenshot. You'll use the same pass every time.

If the page says "This pass is no longer valid," you've been taken off the list.

## At the Door

Open your pass and let the door staff scan the QR code. Once you're checked in, the same pass won't work again for that show.
