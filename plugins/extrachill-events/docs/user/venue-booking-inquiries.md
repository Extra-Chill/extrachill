# Venue Booking Inquiries

Want to play a room? Some venues take booking inquiries through Extra Chill. You can check a date, pitch your show, and track the venue's answer in one place.

If a venue takes inquiries, its page on [Extra Chill Events](https://events.extrachill.com/) shows a **Submit a booking inquiry** button. Some venues also put the same form on their own website.

## Send an Inquiry

You don't need an Extra Chill account to send one.

1. Open the venue's page and select **Submit a booking inquiry**.
2. Select **Start a booking inquiry**.
3. Pick a **Requested date**, and a **Requested space** if the venue has more than one room. Select **Check availability**.
4. If the date is open, fill in your artist or band name, your name, and your email. A phone number is optional.
5. Tell the venue your vision for the show and answer any questions the venue added.
6. Agree to the venue's terms, complete the security check, and select the send button.

Your inquiry goes only to the venue. It never appears on the public venue page.

If the date is taken, the form tells you right away so you can pick another one.

Your answers are saved as you type. If you leave and come back in the same browser, they're restored. If your connection drops while sending, your details stay on the page. Send again and the inquiry won't be created twice.

## After You Send It

You'll see an **Inquiry received** screen with your reference. A confirmation email should arrive within a few minutes, so check your spam folder if you don't see it.

The email includes your reference. If you sent the inquiry while logged out, it also includes a private access code.

Sending an inquiry doesn't hold the date or confirm the booking. To talk with the venue, reply to the confirmation email. Your replies stay in the same booking conversation.

## Check Your Inquiry

Go back to the venue's page. If you're using the same browser, your inquiry opens automatically.

On another device:

1. Open the venue's page. Below the **Start a booking inquiry** button, select **Recover an existing inquiry**.
2. Enter the **Confirmation reference** from your email.
3. If you're logged in with the account you used, that's all you need. Otherwise, enter the **Access code** from the email too.
4. Select **Open inquiry**.

Lost the email? Under **Need a new access email?**, enter your reference and contact email, then select **Email my access code**. For your privacy, we never say whether the details matched. If they did, the email arrives shortly.

## Where Your Inquiry Stands

- **Pending review:** the venue has your inquiry.
- **More information needed:** the venue has a question for you.
- **Under review:** the venue is deciding whether the show fits.
- **In discussion:** you and the venue are working out the details.
- **Date held:** the venue is holding the date for you.
- **Confirmed:** the show is on.
- **Completed:** the show has happened.
- **Declined**, **Withdrawn**, or **Cancelled:** the inquiry is closed.

If someone else books your date before the venue answers, you'll get an email. Your inquiry isn't declined. Reply with another date that works.

## Changes and Withdrawals

From your inquiry, you can:

- **Request a correction.** This sends the venue a request. It doesn't quietly change what you already sent.
- **Withdraw your inquiry.** You can do this any time before the show is confirmed. You'll be asked to confirm first.
- **Request cancellation** once a show is confirmed. The venue decides. Your booking isn't cancelled right away.

Once an inquiry is closed or completed, there's nothing left to change.
