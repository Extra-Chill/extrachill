# Submit a Show

Help fans find a show by sending its details to the Events Calendar. A clear flyer can save you from typing some of those details.

## Send One Event

1. Open [Submit Event](https://events.extrachill.com/submit/).
2. If you are not signed in, enter your name and email so the team can follow up. A free account is set up for that email, and you will receive a link to choose a password.
3. Upload a JPG, PNG, WebP, or PDF flyer if you have one. The team can use it to read the event title, date, and venue.
4. Enter the event title and date. These are required unless you attach a flyer. Add the show time, venue, city or region, lineup, ticket or information link, and any other details you know.
5. Complete the security check and send the event.

When you enter a venue, choose a matching suggestion if one appears. If the venue is not listed, type its name.

## What Happens Next

The team reviews submissions before they appear on the calendar. Sending an event does not mean it will appear right away. Please do not send the same show more than once.

If you are signed in, the form can also test a page that lists several upcoming dates for one artist or one venue. See [Add Your Tour or Venue Calendar](add-your-tour.md). Use the form above for a single show.

## Correct an Event

If a pending or published listing needs a correction, share its event page and the right details in [The Lab](https://community.extrachill.com/r/the-lab), the community help forum.

## After It Appears

Share the event page with your audience. Signed-in fans can mark the show Going, add it to My Shows, and receive an in-app reminder before the event.
