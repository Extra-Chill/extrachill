# Handling Booking Inquiries

Artists who want to play your room can send you a booking inquiry from your venue page. Every request, held date, and confirmed show lands in one place, so your team never double-books a night.

To see what artists see when they send an inquiry, read [Venue Booking Inquiries](venue-booking-inquiries.md). To set up your form and booking rules, read [Setting Up Your Booking Form](setting-up-your-booking-form.md).

Booking inquiries are open to a small number of venues right now. To take inquiries for your room, [contact Extra Chill](https://extrachill.com/contact-us/).

## Who Can Handle Bookings

Everyone on your venue's team can read and answer inquiries, hold dates, and confirm shows. To add people, see [Managing a Venue](managing-a-venue.md).

## Open Your Bookings

1. Log in and open [Venue settings](https://events.extrachill.com/venue-settings/).
2. Pick your venue.
3. Open the **Bookings** tab.

At the top, you'll see how many inquiries are **New submissions**, how many **Needs info**, and how many dates are on hold. If any holds are about to run out, you'll see **Expiring holds** too.

### Calendar or List

- **Calendar** shows a month at a time. Requests, held dates, and confirmed shows all appear together. Use **Previous**, **Today**, and **Next** to move around.
- **List** shows every inquiry as a card. Search by artist, contact name, email, or booking number. Use **Status** to show **Active inquiries**, **Confirmed shows**, or **Closed inquiries**.

Select any inquiry to open it.

## Read an Inquiry

The inquiry shows the artist, the date they asked for, the set time, the room, and their contact details. Their answers to your form questions are there too.

If another artist asked for the same date and room, you'll see a warning. Check the calendar before you confirm.

## Move an Inquiry Along

Each inquiry has a status. To change it:

1. Under **Status**, choose the next step.
2. Add a note if you want one.
3. Select **Apply transition**.

Here's what each status means. The artist sees a matching status when they check their inquiry.

- **Submitted:** a new inquiry you haven't acted on yet.
- **Needs info:** you have a question for the artist.
- **Under review:** you're deciding whether the show fits.
- **Negotiating:** you and the artist are working out the details.
- **Held:** you're holding the date for this artist.
- **Confirmed:** the show is on.
- **Completed:** the show happened.
- **Declined**, **Withdrawn**, or **Cancelled:** the inquiry is closed.

You'll only see the steps that make sense next. For example, you can't confirm a show that's still brand new. You have to review it and talk terms first. Once an inquiry is closed or completed, it can't change again.

## Hold a Date

A hold saves a date and room for one artist while you finish talking. Nobody else can be held or confirmed for that slot until the hold ends.

1. Move the inquiry to **Under review** or **Negotiating**.
2. Under **Performance and holds**, pick the **Space** and set the start and end times. Times use your venue's time zone.
3. Select **Save performance**.
4. Select **Create hold**.
5. Move the inquiry to **Held**.

Each hold lasts as long as your default hold time, which you set on the **Booking Rules** tab. When it runs out, the date opens up again and your owners get a notice.

To let a date go, move the inquiry out of **Held** first. That releases the hold for you.

## Confirm a Show

1. Make sure the inquiry has an active hold.
2. Under **Deal terms**, fill in what you agreed to, like a guarantee or a door split. Select **Save deal terms**.
3. Move the inquiry to **Confirmed**.

When you confirm a show, any other artists who asked for the same date get an email. It tells them the date is filled and invites them to reply with another date. Their inquiries aren't declined.

You can also save **Production details**, like what the artist needs and what your venue offers.

## Put the Show on the Calendar

Once a show is confirmed, select **Convert to event**. The show is added to your venue's calendar on Extra Chill. If something goes wrong, you'll see a retry button.

## Email the Artist

At the bottom of an inquiry, under **Correspondence**, you can email the artist without leaving the page.

1. Add a **Subject**.
2. Enter your **Reply-to email**. That's where the artist's answer will go.
3. Write your **Message**.
4. Select the button that starts with **Email** followed by the artist's address.

Your venue team gets a copy. Every message you send is listed under **Correspondence** so the whole team can see what was said.

When the artist replies, their answer goes to the reply-to email you entered. It doesn't show up inside the inquiry, so keep an eye on that inbox.

## How You Hear About New Inquiries

- **Email.** When a new inquiry arrives, everyone on your venue team gets an email. Reply to it to write back to the artist directly.
- **Notifications.** Venue owners get a notification on Extra Chill when an inquiry arrives, when an artist answers your question, when a hold runs out, and when an artist asks for a change, asks to cancel, or withdraws.

If you don't open a notification for a while, we'll email you a reminder. You can turn these reminder emails off in your notification settings, or with the unsubscribe link in the email.

## Files From Artists

Some venues will be able to collect files like a press kit or a stage plot with each inquiry. This isn't open yet. For now, ask artists to send links instead.

## Privacy and How Long We Keep Inquiry Details

Inquiries are private. They never appear on your public venue page. Only your venue team, the artist, and the Extra Chill team can see them.

We don't keep personal details forever. After a while, we remove the artist's name, email, and answers from old inquiries, and then remove the messages.

- **Declined or withdrawn inquiries:** contact details and answers are removed after about 6 months, and messages after about 1 year.
- **Open inquiries:** contact details, answers, and messages are removed after about 2 years.
- **Confirmed, cancelled, or completed shows:** contact details and answers are removed after about 2 years, and messages after about 3 years.

The basic record that a booking happened stays longer, so your history still adds up.

An artist can ask us for a copy of their inquiry details or ask us to erase them. If they sent an inquiry without an account, we match it by the exact email they used.
