# Weekly Local Scene Email

Never miss a good show in your city. The weekly Local Scene email lands once a week with upcoming shows in one city, so you can plan your week from your inbox.

## What You Get

- **One email a week** called "Your Local Scene: This Week's Shows."
- **Up to eight shows** happening over the next seven days in that city.
- **Your plans first.** Shows you've marked Going are listed under **You're Going**. The rest are under **More This Week**.
- **A See Every Show button** that opens the city's full calendar.
- **A matching notification** on Extra Chill that says "Your Local Scene weekly picks are ready."

If a city has no upcoming shows that week, no email goes out.

## Sign Up with Just Your Email

You don't need to be signed in. Look for the weekly email offer on a city's calendar page or on any show in that city.

1. Open a city on the [Events Calendar](https://events.extrachill.com/location), or open a show in that city.
2. Find **Get this week's shows in** your city **by email** on a show, or **Get** your city **shows in your inbox** on a city page.
3. Type your email and tap **Get this week's shows**.
4. Check your inbox for an email called "Confirm to get this week's shows in" your city.
5. Tap **Confirm**, then tap **Yes, send me the weekly email** on the page that opens.

Nothing starts until you confirm. If someone else typed your email, ignore the message and nothing happens. The confirmation link works for seven days. After that, sign up again from any city page.

If you're new to Extra Chill, the confirmation email also has a **Set your password** link. We made you a free account with that email, so you can sign in later to save shows and change your settings. Confirming also saves the city as your Local Scene if you didn't have one yet.

## Sign Up When You're Signed In

When you're signed in, these buttons sign you up for your Local Scene, the city you've picked as home on Extra Chill. Learn how to pick it in [Your Local Scene](https://docs.extrachill.com/accounts/local-scene).

**From a show:** open any show in your city and tap **Email me the weekly shows**. If you haven't picked a Local Scene yet, this also saves that city as yours. If your Local Scene is a different city, the offer doesn't appear on that show.

**From a city page:** open your city's calendar page and tap **Subscribe to email + updates**. The button changes to **Subscribed to email + updates** once it's on. On a city that isn't your Local Scene, tap **Make this my city** first.

**From your settings:**

1. Open your [account settings](https://community.extrachill.com/settings) and go to **Notifications**.
2. Check **Weekly** your city **Local Scene digest**.
3. Tap **Save Preferences**.

If you haven't picked a Local Scene yet, the Notifications tab asks you to choose one in **Account Details** first.

**Signing in from an offer:** if you tap **Have an account? Sign in** or **Sign in for the weekly email**, you come back to the same page after signing in. Tap **Confirm: send me the weekly email** or **Confirm: save and subscribe** to finish. Nothing changes until you tap it.

## Make Sure the Email Arrives

- **Email notifications need to be on.** In [account settings](https://community.extrachill.com/settings) under **Notifications**, keep **Email notifications** checked. With it off, you still get the notification on Extra Chill but not the email.
- **The email follows the city you signed up for.** Each weekly email covers the city you signed up for, even if your Local Scene is a different city. Changing your Local Scene doesn't stop or move it. To switch cities, unsubscribe from the old city and sign up for the new one. Your signed-up cities are listed under **Subscriptions** in your settings.
- **Check your spam folder** if you confirmed but don't see the email after a week.

## Unsubscribe

You can stop the weekly email at any time.

- **From the email:** tap **Unsubscribe from this Local Scene digest** at the bottom, then tap **Unsubscribe from this Local Scene** on the page that opens.
- **From a city page:** on your city's calendar page, tap **Subscribed to email + updates** to turn it off.
- **From your settings:** under **Notifications**, uncheck the weekly digest and tap **Save Preferences**. You can also tap **Unsubscribe** next to it under **Subscriptions**.

Unsubscribing stops both the weekly email and its notification for that city. Your Local Scene and your other notification settings stay the same.
