# Inviting Bands to the Guest List

Let each band or their manager put their own people on the list. They get a private link and type names in themselves. No account and no password needed.

The show's guest list has to be on before you can invite anyone. See [Hosting a Guest List](hosting-a-guest-list.md).

## Send an Invite

1. On the show's page, find **Invite a band** under **Manage guest list**.
2. Enter the **Band or management name** and the **Email for the invite link**.
3. If each band gets a set number of spots, fill in **Spots for this band**.
4. Tap **Send invite**.

The band gets an email called "Add your guest list for" the show, with an **Add guest names** button. You'll also see their invite link right away. Tap **Copy invite link** if you want to text it too. This is the only time the link is shown, so copy it now if you need it.

Each invite in your list shows how many names the band has added. If bands get a set number of spots, you can change a band's number and tap **Save spots**.

## If a Band Loses Their Link

The link can't be shown again. Tap **Revoke** next to the band, then send a new invite to the same email.

When you revoke an invite:

- The old link stops working.
- Guests the band already added stay on the list and keep their passes.
- Names still waiting for your approval are dropped.

## For Bands: Adding Your Names

1. Open the link from your invite email.
2. Type a **Guest name**. Add their email if you want them to get the pass by email.
3. Tap **Add to guest list**.
4. Tap **Copy pass link** and text it to your guest.

Under **Your names** you'll see everyone you added. Each name shows its pass link, or says "checked in," "waiting for the venue," or "not approved by the venue." Tap **Remove** to take someone off before they check in.

At the door, your guests show up with your band's name next to theirs, like "Jake (Slim S.O.U.L.)".

Keep the link private. Anyone with it can add names. If the venue cancels your invite, the page says so and you'll need to contact them.

## For Bands: Asking for More

When your spots run out, the page says "You've used all your guest list spots." If the whole show fills up, it says "The guest list is full." Either way, you can ask the venue.

**Ask for more spots.** This only appears when the venue gives each band a set number of spots.

1. Open **Need more spots? Ask the venue**.
2. Enter the number of **Extra spots** and an optional note.
3. Tap **Ask for more spots**.

You can have one request waiting at a time. The venue emails you when they decide.

**Send a whole list.** This works on any invite.

1. Open **Send a whole list for the venue to approve**.
2. Paste one name per line, up to 50 at a time.
3. Tap **Send list for approval**.

Those names show as "waiting for the venue." Their passes don't work at the door until the venue approves them.

## Not Invited? Request Spots

Bands and managers without an invite can ask from the show's guest list page.

1. Open the guest list page the venue shared.
2. Open **Playing this show? Request guest list spots**.
3. Enter your band or management name, your email, and how many spots you need.
4. Add guest names, one per line, and a note if you like.
5. Tap **Send request**.

Each email address can have one request waiting per show. If the venue accepts, you'll get your own private invite link by email.

## Approving and Declining Requests

When a band asks for something, you get an email and the request appears under **Pending requests** on the show's page. Nothing in a request works at the door until you accept it.

Each request says what the band wants:

- **Not invited, asks for spots:** a band without an invite. If bands get a set number of spots, adjust **Spots for this band** first. When you tap **Accept**, they get their own invite link by email, and you see the link to copy.
- **Asks for more spots:** adjust **Extra spots** if you want to give a different number, then tap **Accept**.
- **Sent names for approval:** the names are listed. Tap **Accept** to put them all on the list.

**Lists are all or nothing.** If there aren't enough spots left on the show for every name, none of them go on, and you'll see a message saying so. Raise the show's guest list spots or ask the band to take names off, then accept again. If the band gets a set number of spots, approving their list raises their number to fit.

If you accept a band that wasn't invited but their names don't all fit, the band still gets its invite link. The names stay waiting under **Pending requests** for you to decide later.

Tap **Deny** to say no. The band gets an email either way. Denying a request for more spots leaves their current spots as they were.
