# Promoter Organizations

Put on shows around town? A verified promoter organization gives your crew its own home on Extra Chill: a shared Link Page, a team, and guest lists for every show you put on.

## Get Verified

The Extra Chill team verifies every promoter organization by hand. To get yours set up, [contact Extra Chill](https://extrachill.com/contact-us/). Tell us who should be the first owner.

## Your Promoter Team

Everyone on your promoter team is either a member or an organization owner.

- **Members** can work in your promoter workspace and run guest lists for your shows.
- **Organization owners** can also manage your money, like ticket sales and refunds.

To add someone to your team or remove them, [contact Extra Chill](https://extrachill.com/contact-us/). Every organization keeps at least one owner, so the last owner can't leave until someone else takes over.

## Open Your Promoter Workspace

1. Log in and open [Venue settings](https://events.extrachill.com/venue-settings/).
2. Under **Active identity**, pick your organization from **Promoter organizations**.

Switching identities only changes what you're looking at. It doesn't change what you're allowed to do.

Your workspace shows your organization's name and your role, either **Active member** or **Organization owner**.

## Your Promoter Link Page

Your organization gets its own Link Page: one simple page for all your links that you can share anywhere. Edit it right in your promoter workspace.

## Shows You Put On

When a show on the [Events Calendar](https://events.extrachill.com/) lists your organization as its promoter, your team can run that show's guest list and door list. Log in and open the show's page to get started.

You can also keep a standing list of people who get into every one of your shows. See:

- [Venue and Promoter Guest Lists](venue-and-promoter-guest-lists.md)
- [Hosting a Guest List](hosting-a-guest-list.md)
- [Working the Door](working-the-door.md)

## Working With Venues

A venue can let your organization help with certain things at that venue. Right now, the one thing a venue can share with you is organizing local support for their shows.

Venues that work with you are listed under **Granted venues** in your workspace. If none are listed yet, you'll see "This promoter has no active venue grants."

The venue stays in charge of its own settings, booking inquiries, team, and money. Your organization can't see or change those.

## Ticket Sales

Organization owners see a ticket sales area in the workspace. Selling tickets through Extra Chill isn't open to promoters yet. For now, use your guest list to manage who gets in.
