# Using Calendar Filters

If you followed **How does this work?** from an artist’s tour dates page, the calendar shows that artist’s upcoming shows listed on Extra Chill. The line above the calendar (for example, “11 upcoming events at 10 venues in 10 locations”) describes the whole artist page, not just your filtered results.

Venue pages show dates at that room, location pages show dates in that city or region, and festival pages bring together listings for that festival. The [All Live Music Events calendar](https://events.extrachill.com/all) covers all locations. Listings are updated as Extra Chill imports dates from sources such as venue and promoter calendars and publishes approved event submissions.

## Search or Pick Dates

- **Search events...**: Type an event, artist, or venue name to look within the current calendar. Results update after you pause typing, or you can click the magnifying glass.
- **Select date range...**: Pick a start and end date. The list updates once both dates are selected. Use the **✕** next to the date field to clear just the date range.

Use the time chips for a shortcut. They change the dates shown without taking you to a different calendar:

- **All** removes the time preset but keeps your search, date range, and other filters.
- **Today** shows today’s listings.
- **Tonight** narrows the list to evening shows, starting at 5 p.m. or later.
- **This Weekend** covers Friday through Sunday, or the rest of the weekend if it has already started.
- **This Week** covers today and the next six days.

If you pick both a date range and a time preset, results must match both.

## Filter by Location, Genre, or Event Type

Open **Filter** to see **Event Display Filters**. The sections and choices depend on what matches the current calendar. For example, an artist page may show just **Locations** and **Event Types**, while the full calendar can also show **Genres**.

- **Locations** narrows the list by city or region.
- **Genres** narrows it by the performers’ music genres.
- **Event Types** narrows it by the kind of event, such as **Open Mic**.

Check the options you want, then choose **Apply Filters** to update the calendar. Choose **Cancel** to close without applying your changes.

The calendar can remember your choices from the **Filter** window in this browser. Choose **Clear All Filters** to remove those saved choices and reset search, dates, and the time preset. You stay on the same page: clearing filters on an artist page still shows only that artist’s listings.

## See More Results

When there are more matches, choose **Load More Events** below the list to add the next batch without changing your filters. If you see numbered pages instead, use a page number or **Next** to continue.

## Open or Save a Show

Choose **More Info** on a listing to open the event page, where you’ll find ticket options when available. Sign in and mark the show **Going** to save it to [My Shows](https://events.extrachill.com/my-shows/).

For more ways to browse, see [Finding Live Music](https://docs.extrachill.com/events-calendar/finding-live-music) or open [Live Music Near Me](https://events.extrachill.com/near-me).
