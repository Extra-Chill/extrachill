# Managing a Venue

Run a room? Manage your venue on Extra Chill and keep your page, your calendar, and your team in one place.

Everything starts on the [Venue settings](https://events.extrachill.com/venue-settings/) page. You need to be logged in to use it.

Venue tools are open to a small number of venues right now. To get your venue started, [contact Extra Chill](https://extrachill.com/contact-us/).

## Get Access to Your Venue

There are two ways in.

### Claim Your Venue

If your venue is already listed on the [Events Calendar](https://events.extrachill.com/), you can claim it.

1. Log in and open [Venue settings](https://events.extrachill.com/venue-settings/).
2. Under **Claim your venue**, choose your venue from the list.
3. Select **Submit claim**.

You'll see a thank-you message. Our team reviews every claim before handing over the keys. Once your claim is approved, you become the venue's first owner.

Don't see your venue in the list? Select **Tell us about it** and we'll add it.

### Accept an Invitation

If someone on your venue's team invites you, you'll get an email called **Invitation to manage** followed by the venue name.

1. Open the email and select **Accept Invitation**.
2. Log in if you're asked to.
3. You'll land on the venue's page, ready to go.

New to Extra Chill? The button says **Set Your Password** instead. Set your password first, then accept the invitation.

Invitations last 7 days. If yours runs out, ask the person who invited you to send it again.

## Members and Owners

Everyone on a venue's team is either a member or an owner.

- **Members** can work on the venue: its page, calendar, graphics, Link Page, and booking inquiries.
- **Owners** can do all of that, plus manage the team and the venue's money, like refunds for ticket sales.

## Manage Your Team

Owners see a **Team** tab.

### Invite Someone

1. Open the **Team** tab.
2. Under **Invite a teammate**, enter their **Email**.
3. Check **Venue owner (can manage team)** if they should be an owner.
4. Select **Send invitation**.

They won't have access until they accept the email invitation.

If someone already has an invitation, a claim, or a spot on the team, you can't invite them again.

### Keep Track of Invitations

The **Invitations** list shows each invitation you've sent and where it stands. While an invitation is still waiting, you can:

- Select **Resend** to send the email again.
- Select **Cancel** to take the invitation back.

### Change or Remove Access

The **Memberships** list shows everyone on your team. Next to each active person:

- Select **Make owner** to give them owner access, or **Make member** to take it away.
- Select **Revoke** to remove them from the venue. You'll be asked to confirm first.

## Your Venue Tabs

When you open a venue, you'll see these tabs.

### Bookings, Booking Form, and Booking Rules

Take show requests from artists, set up the form they fill out, and choose how dates get held. See [Handling Booking Inquiries](handling-booking-inquiries.md) and [Setting Up Your Booking Form](setting-up-your-booking-form.md).

### Venue

Update your **Public venue profile**: your venue name, description, address, phone, website, and capacity. Only the venue name is required. These details show on your venue page.

### Calendar Feed

Connect your venue's own calendar so your shows come to Extra Chill on their own.

1. Copy the public address of your calendar. In most calendar apps, look in the calendar's settings for a public or shareable address ending in .ics.
2. Paste it into **Public calendar address**.
3. Select **Connect calendar**.

The calendar must be public. Shows marked private, confidential, tentative, or cancelled are never brought in. Everything else is added for review first, so nothing goes public until someone approves it.

Select **Import now** to check for new shows right away, or **Disconnect** to stop.

### Graphics

Turn your upcoming shows into slides ready to post on Instagram.

1. Pick a range: **This week**, **This month**, **Next month**, or **Custom**.
2. Pick a layout: **List by day** or **Compact calendar**.
3. Add a title if you like.
4. Select **Generate graphics**, then **Download all**.

Slides only include shows already live on the Extra Chill calendar. They carry your venue logo when one is set.

### Link Page

Edit your venue's Link Page: one simple page with all your links that you can share anywhere.

### Tickets

See ticket sales for your shows on Extra Chill. Selling tickets through Extra Chill isn't open to venues yet. For now, use your guest list to manage who gets in. See [Hosting a Guest List](hosting-a-guest-list.md).

## Guest Lists for Your Venue

Your team can run guest lists for your shows and keep a standing list of people who always get in. See [Venue and Promoter Guest Lists](venue-and-promoter-guest-lists.md) and [Working the Door](working-the-door.md).
