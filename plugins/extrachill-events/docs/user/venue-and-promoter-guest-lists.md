# Venue and Promoter Guest Lists

Some people always get in: partners, friends, your regular sound engineer. Put them on your standing list once and they get one pass that works at every one of your shows.

## Who Can Manage It

A venue's team manages the venue list. A promoter's team manages the promoter list. Log in and open any of your shows on the [Events Calendar](https://events.extrachill.com/). Your list appears on the show's page as **Owner's list for** your venue or promoter name.

If a show lists both your venue and a promoter, each team sees its own list.

## How It Works

- **It works at every show.** The pass is good at every show listed under your venue or promoter on Extra Chill, for as long as the person stays on the list.
- **It doesn't use spots.** People on your standing list never use one of a show's guest list spots.
- **It works without a show guest list.** Your standing list still works when a show's own guest list is off.
- **One check-in per show.** The same pass checks in once tonight, then again at your next show.

## Add Someone

1. Under **Add someone**, type their **Name**.
2. Add their email if you have it. They'll get their pass by email.
3. Tap **Add to list**.

You'll see "Added" with their pass link. Tap **Copy pass link** and text it to them so they have it at the door. Each person on the list keeps a pass link you can copy again any time.

## Take Someone Off

Tap **Remove** next to their name. Their pass stops working right away, at every show.

## At the Door

Door staff scan the person's pass to check them in. People on your list also show up on the door list with "(Venue list)" or "(Promoter list)" after their name. See [Working the Door](working-the-door.md).

For what your guests see, see [Getting on a Guest List](getting-on-a-guest-list.md).
